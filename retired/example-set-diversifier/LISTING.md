# example-set-diversifier

Unpublished original; editorial tier: paid-candidate. No public score or release approval.

## What and when

Use when repetitive teaching examples must be redesigned around a supplied learning objective and explicit decision rules, including normal, counter, missing, conflicting, or requested undetermined cases. Do not use for generic editing, full curriculum creation, invented system behavior, unsupported case studies, or accessibility certification.

## Task boundary

study-and-teaching-kit builds learning materials, and classroom-quiz-builder creates source-grounded assessment questions. example-set-diversifier instead redesigns supplied teaching examples around explicit decision rules, coverage signatures, minimal pairs, and genuinely undetermined cases without creating a full curriculum or quiz.
