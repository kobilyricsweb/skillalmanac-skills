# scope-change-brief

Status: unpublished free candidate; no score or verification badge.

## One Line

Turn an accepted baseline and requested change into a traceable scope delta brief.

## Audience

Product owners, project managers, analysts, and reviewers.

## Benefit

Makes changed deliverables, dependencies, acceptance, and approval gaps explicit.

## Limits

It compares supplied material only; it does not estimate, approve, specify a whole product, or execute work.

## Suggested Example

Fictional example: compare a baseline CSV export with a request to add JSON and a new field.

## Evidence

See VERIFICATION.md. Examples are synthetic, not customer outcomes.
