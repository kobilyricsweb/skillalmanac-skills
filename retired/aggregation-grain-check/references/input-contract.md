Required input fields are: table names; row keys or declared row identity; measure definitions and current grain; join keys and relationship notes; intended grouping keys; filters; deduplication rules; and proposed aggregation order. State whether measures are additive at the intended grain. Missing keys or relationship cardinality prevent a safe declaration and must be reported as unresolved. A non-additive or snapshot measure crossing its declared grain is unsafe even when joins do not duplicate rows. If a deduplication rule is supplied, include its selection basis and scope; do not invent one.

The output must contain: a table-grain worksheet; measure-grain analysis; the proposed join, filter, grouping, and aggregation sequence; risks; supported or conditional alternatives; and verification findings. Each measure has an overall status of safe, unsafe, or unresolved plus a structured hazards array. Record `duplication` whenever a join can repeat the measure row and `non_additivity` whenever aggregation crosses its declared non-additive grain. Record both when both apply.

Preserve proposed operations in the order stated. Do not reorder them for optimization or replace them with a remedy. If predicate placement is unclear, preserve the wording and report the ambiguity. A repeated measure row can duplicate totals; uniqueness must be evidenced at the relevant grain; aggregation before a one-to-many join is only a proposal unless supported by supplied intent; and the artifact assesses structural safety, not observed numerical error.
