# Input Contract

Baseline fields to collect:

- Assessment format and standard conditions.
- Assessed construct or learning goal.
- Item content or item-set description.
- Response demand or response mode.
- Scoring target or scoring rule.
- Standard timing and materials.
- Available session, room, staffing, and equipment constraints.

Required authorization:

- Exact approved accommodation wording.

If approval wording or constraints are missing, identify the gap and do not invent an operational change. If the construct, item content, response demand, scoring target, or another baseline element is missing, list each missing element separately.

Known facts may still support partial work. For example, supplied timing can be calculated from an exact extra-time approval, and supplied format or materials can be reported unchanged. Keep that unchanged-form comparison separate from construct fidelity: preservation of the intended construct remains unverified until the construct and all baselines needed for that judgment are supplied.

If an approval conflicts with a constraint, mark the implementation unresolved or conflicting and state who or what must decide. Availability alone does not establish that a resource has an approved property such as low distraction. Do not infer eligibility, diagnosis, legal status, privacy facts, or private student information.

Output fields:

- Baseline conditions, using unresolved or null values for missing facts.
- Accommodation-to-logistics implementation records.
- Calculated timing and materials.
- Preserved supplied elements.
- Unresolved logistics and missing baselines.
- Assumptions and traceability checklist.

Use only approved accommodations and supplied learning goals. Proposals remain provisional until the relevant authority confirms them.
