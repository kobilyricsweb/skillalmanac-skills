Required fields: archive inventory; folder and item descriptions; dates or date spans when supplied; identifiers; hierarchy rules; and access annotations. A complete inventory should distinguish folders from items and preserve source identifiers. If a required field is missing, state the limitation and produce only entries supported by the available data; do not infer placement or contents. Output fields: hierarchy; series and subgroup entries; date spans; item scope descriptions; identifiers; access notes; uncertain or unclassified section; coverage check; and unresolved questions. Use date spans only when supported by supplied dates. “Unclassified” means placement is unsupported, not that the item lacks content. The finding aid describes a fixed archive and does not authorize physical or digital reorganization.
