# Brief: Elm Court Apartments (fictional)

- Product: 40-unit apartment building in Denver, CO. Two-bedrooms from 1,850 a month, one-bedrooms from 1,450.
- Goal: book tours. A booked tour is the thank-you page /tour/confirmed. About 1 in 3 tours becomes a lease.
- Budget: 900 for 30 days on Meta (Facebook and Instagram). Search is not wanted.
- Landing page: https://elmcourt.example/tour
- Marketing wants to aim at "young professionals, 25 to 34, mostly women" and mention it is great for small families too.
- Estimated cost per click 1.50; 5 percent of clicks book a tour.
