## Before spending

```
- [ ] Validator output: valid true, warnings read and accepted or fixed
- [ ] Conversion tested with a real submission and visible in analytics
- [ ] Every landing URL opens over https, loads on mobile in under about 3 seconds, matches the ad promise
- [ ] Landing page states every claim used in the copy (price, guarantee terms, qualifications)
- [ ] Privacy policy and contact details present on the landing site
- [ ] Billing set up by the user; monthly cap equals total_budget in the plan
- [ ] Special-category flag set if housing, credit or employment
- [ ] Policy red-flag table read for the vertical; images and video checked by a human
```

## Campaign set-up

```
- [ ] Search: location setting is "presence" (people in the area), not "presence or interest", unless travel is the product
- [ ] Search: partner or display network expansion off for the first month (extra reach, weak intent)
- [ ] Search: negative keywords loaded at campaign level before the first ad is enabled
- [ ] Social: placements checked; unneeded placements off for the first test
- [ ] Ad schedule matches when the business can answer (calls and quotes)
- [ ] Daily budget equals plan daily_budget; no budget sharing between campaigns during the test
- [ ] Bid strategy equals the plan; automated bidding only if 30 or more conversions are forecast
- [ ] Tracking template or UTMs set; final URLs equal the plan
- [ ] Ad rotation and asset counts meet the platform minimum
```

These setting names are as the platforms commonly label them, verify (unconfirmed, as of 2026-11); the intent matters more than the label.

## First 72 hours

```
- [ ] Day 0: all ads approved or in review; fix disapprovals by editing the copy, not by resubmitting the same text
- [ ] Day 1: spend on pace (within 20 percent of daily budget), impressions exist
- [ ] Day 1: search terms report opened; add obvious waste as negatives
- [ ] Day 3: first conversions tracked and attributed correctly; if clicks exist but zero conversions after 3 x target CPA spent, check the conversion tag and the landing page before changing the ads
```

## Stop conditions

Pause the campaign and tell the user when: spend runs above 130 percent of the daily budget two days running, a policy warning threatens the account, the landing page is down or the form is broken, or lead quality is bad (for example more than half of leads are spam). Do not raise budgets in the first week unless CPA is under target on at least 15 conversions.
