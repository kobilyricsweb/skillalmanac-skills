Required fields:
- Complete passage text, including paragraph or section breaks when available.
- Learning goal.
- Permitted question levels or cognitive moves.
- Expected response length.

The source must identify whether reflection questions are allowed. If the passage is incomplete, a required level is not permitted, or response length is missing, state the limitation and produce only the supportable portion. If wording is ambiguous, quote or point to the relevant passage location without resolving it from outside knowledge.

Output fields:
- Ordered question ladder.
- Passage location for every question.
- Cognitive move.
- Expected evidence and response length.
- Answerability check.
- Limits or reflection label.

Every factual question must be answerable from the passage. Evaluation may ask what the passage proves or leaves unproven, but must not add external facts.
