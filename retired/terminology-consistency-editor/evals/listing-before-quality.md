# terminology-consistency-editor

Unpublished original review draft. Editorial tier: free. No Platform Score or public sale.

## One Line

Identify and localize true terminology inconsistencies while preserving meaning.

## Audience

Editors managing glossary-driven multi-author content.

## Deliverable

Preferred-terms table and location-specific replacement list.

## Limits

Requires supplied glossary; preserves quotations and code; no factual reconciliation or broad copyedit.

Scope distinction: Unlike natural-prose-editor and translation-and-localization, this applies an explicit supplied preferred glossary to bounded editable prose, not a general style rewrite or translation.
