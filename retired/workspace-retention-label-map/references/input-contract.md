Required inputs are document-type definitions; the approved retention-label list; explicit mapping rules; exclusions or an explicit statement that no exclusions apply; and synthetic file descriptions. Descriptions may omit cues required for classification, such as signed or draft status; such omissions are valid inputs and must produce unresolved manual-review cases rather than input-contract failures. Include conflict handling and any manual-review policy. Missing cues, ambiguous descriptions, and absent labels must remain unresolved.

Return fields for each description: document description, candidate type, defining cues, assigned supplied label or unknown, applicable rule, conflict, exclusion, and manual-review status. Include an approved-label check, unresolved queue, rule register, and exclusion register. Record exclusion IDs and exact supplied exclusion rules, or record the supplied no-exclusions statement. Do not infer legal requirements or retention periods, inspect files, or perform file operations. A map is a proposal and does not authorize changes.
