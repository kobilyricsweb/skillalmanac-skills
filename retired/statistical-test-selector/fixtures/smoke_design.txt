Study: two independent groups compared on a single session score.
Outcome: continuous score, ratio scale, 0 to 100.
Groups: control (n = 14) and training (n = 16). Participants were allocated at random and no participant appears in both groups.
Observations: one score per participant.
Distribution: scores are right-skewed; sample skewness is about 1.4 in the control group and about 1.2 in the training group.
Group standard deviations differ by a ratio of about 2.6.
Question: is the training group's score higher than the control group's?
Design: randomised experiment.
