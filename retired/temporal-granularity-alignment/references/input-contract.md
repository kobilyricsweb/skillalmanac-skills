Required fields are: timestamp or period fields for each source; source time zones; calendar definitions; intended comparison grain; and approved aggregation rules. Include boundary conventions, such as inclusive dates or month definitions, plus known gaps or incomplete periods. If a source lacks a timezone, calendar, grain, or aggregation rule needed for the requested alignment, mark the affected comparison unresolved rather than choosing silently. The output must state each source’s native grain, target grain, permitted rollups, forbidden expansions, boundary conventions, gap handling, and unresolved cases. It must distinguish a plan from an executed join. Coarse observations cannot become finer observations without an explicit allocation rule. Do not invent timestamps, fill gaps, or infer equivalence across calendars.
