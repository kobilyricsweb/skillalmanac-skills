# Input contract

Provide:

- `context`: product or service and use case.
- `outcome_summary`: supplied wording, claims, dates, measures, and sources.
- `interviewee`: role, organization status, and relationship if known.
- `case_audience`: intended readers.
- `topics`: approved areas to explore.
- `restrictions`: anonymity, attribution, quote, channel, or approval constraints.
- `existing_evidence`: records, messages, metrics, or wording already supplied.
- `time_cap`: optional maximum duration for the core interview agenda.

Decision rules:

- Label customer statements, company assumptions, records, and unknowns separately.
- Ask neutral questions; do not presuppose success or causality.
- Request confirmation for material facts and permission for quotations.
- Preserve conflicting accounts and ask for reconciliation.
- Treat absent permission and absent evidence as unresolved.
- If `time_cap` is supplied, allocate opening, questions, evidence, and permission follow-ups within that cap; label allocations as planning estimates and keep extras outside the core agenda.

Boundary:

This produces an interview instrument and evidence-request checklist. It does not create a case study, quote, testimonial, legal conclusion, customer result, compliance determination, or measured feasibility claim.
