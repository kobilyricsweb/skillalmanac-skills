Game idea: "Moss & Mortar" - a cozy tower-restoration game for PC. I am one developer,
16 weeks, about 25 hours a week on it. Players clear rubble from an overgrown tower,
carry stone blocks up stairs to rebuild floors, and earn tokens from visitors who tour
the finished floors. Tokens buy tools (faster carrying, bigger wheelbarrow) and unlock
the next tower. Sessions should be about 20 minutes. Revenue: premium, 8 USD.
I like Unpacking for the calm tone and A Short Hike for the short loop.
Three towers planned; I think each tower should take about 2 hours.
