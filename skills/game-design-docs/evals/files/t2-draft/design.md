# Void Garden

## Core Loop

Loop: plant -> wait

## Pillars

1. Relaxing
2. Deep strategy and mastery of systems
3. Beautiful hand-painted worlds to explore
4. Constant surprise from procedural events

## Mechanics

### Planting

- Inputs: click a plot
- Outputs: a seedling appears

### Watering

- Inputs: hold the can over a plot
- Outputs: soil moisture rises
- Feedback: TBD

### Harvest

- Inputs: click a ripe plant
- Outputs: crop in inventory
- Feedback: crop pops out with a sparkle particle and a pluck sound
