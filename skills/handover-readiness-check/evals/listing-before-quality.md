# handover-readiness-check

Status: unpublished free candidate; no score or verification badge.

## One Line

Assess whether a supplied handoff is reproducible and ready against each stated acceptance criterion.

## Audience

Engineering leads, project owners, QA coordinators, and incoming maintainers.

## Benefit

Separates reported success from inspectable evidence and exposes concrete handoff gaps.

## Limits

Uses supplied material only; it does not deploy, access credentials, audit code, or verify external systems.

## Suggested Example

Fictional example: assess a handoff claiming a PDF export passed while omitting the artifact and inspection record.

## Evidence

See VERIFICATION.md. Examples are synthetic, not customer outcomes.
