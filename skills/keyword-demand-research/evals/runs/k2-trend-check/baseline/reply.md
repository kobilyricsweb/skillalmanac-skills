# Is habit-tracker growing? Short answer: no. Both of his numbers are true, and both mislead.

Data: 9 keyword rows, US monthly, Aug 2025 to Oct 2026 (15 months). "Last month" = Oct 2026.

## What he got right

- **"Rebounded 20%+ last month"**: true. Oct vs Sep is +22% for "habit tracker app" (6,600 to 8,100), and +22% to +30% across every row.
- **"AI angle up thousands of percent YoY"**: true. "ai habit tracker" went from 10 searches (Oct 2025) to 1,300 (Oct 2026), about +12,900%.

## Why neither supports "pour content in now"

**1. The rebound is a bounce off the bottom of a seasonal decline.**
- Every non-AI row spiked in January 2026 (New Year's resolutions) and then fell for eight months. "Habit tracker app" went 40,500 in Jan, then 27.1k, 18.1k, 14.8k, 12.1k, 9.9k, 8.1k, 8.1k, to 6,600 in Sep. That is 84% down from the peak to the Sep trough.
- Oct (8,100) is back to where it was in Jul and Aug. It is still 80% below January.
- The same shape shows up in the 2025 data. Demand climbed from Oct 2025 (9,900) into the Dec/Jan spike, so the Sep-to-Oct uptick looks like the start of the next winter ramp. It is not new growth.
- One month of data cannot separate a trend from seasonality. We have only one January and no full prior year.

**2. Year over year, the core topic is flat to down.**
Oct 2026 vs Oct 2025:
- habit tracker app: -18%
- free habit tracker app: -16%
- how to build a habit tracker in notion: -28%
- best habit tracker app: 0%
- habit tracker widget iphone: 0%
- habit tracker for adhd: +85%, from a small base (390 to 720)

Summed across all 9 rows, Oct 2026 is 25.9k and Oct 2025 was 28.5k, about -9%. Without the AI row it is -14%.

**3. The AI "thousands of percent" figure comes from a near-zero base.**
- It was 0 in Aug and Sep 2025 and 10 in Oct 2025. Any positive number is a huge percentage, so the percentage tells you nothing about size.
- In absolute terms, "ai habit tracker" is 1,300/month. That is about 16% of "habit tracker app" volume and about 5% of the nine-row total.
- It has also peaked. It hit 1,900 in Apr-May and has slid or flattened since: 1,600 in Jun, 1,300 in Jul-Aug, 1,000 in Sep, 1,300 in Oct. That is -32% from peak. It is still 48% above January's 880.
- The AI row is the only genuinely new demand in the file, and it is real. It is also small, and it already follows the same boom-and-fade pattern (spike, fade, small bounce) as the core terms.

## Data caveats

- The head terms are effectively one signal counted several times. "habit tracker app" and "habit tracker apps" are identical in every month, and so are "best habit tracker app" and "best habit tracker apps". The other non-AI rows are the same seasonal shape at a different scale. Do not read the nine rows as nine independent confirmations.
- The values look like rounded or bucketed keyword-tool volumes (9,900, 12,100, 14,800, 40,500...). Month-to-month steps of 20-30% can be one bucket. Treat small moves as noisy.
- Only 15 months of history, one seasonal cycle. The export cannot tell us whether Jan 2026 was a normal peak or an unusually big one.
- The export gives search volume only. It has no information on competition, ability to rank, or monetization. Demand alone does not decide whether to invest.

## What I would tell him

"You're right on both facts, but they don't show the topic is growing. Searches rebounded because it's October and we're heading into the New Year's peak. That is a seasonal pattern, and we're still below last October. The AI term is up a lot in percent but from almost nothing, and it's about 1,300 searches a month, already down from its spring high."

## Practical recommendation

- **Don't frame it as growth.** Frame it as a strong seasonal topic with a roughly 4x peak-to-trough swing (Jan vs Sep).
- **If we want this topic, timing is the argument.** Content published in Nov-Dec has the best chance of ranking before the January spike. That is a calendar reason to publish soon, and it is a different case from "demand is exploding".
- **Treat AI habit tracker as a small, testable niche.** Size it honestly at about 1,300/month and rising off a low base. Run a small pilot (a few pages) rather than a big content push, and check whether the Jan 2027 AI peak is higher than Jan 2026.
- **ADHD looks like the healthier sub-topic.** It is up 85% YoY from a real base and still has its own seasonal swing. It is worth a closer look.
- **Before committing a large budget, get what this export lacks.** Pull 3-5 years of history (Google Trends or a longer keyword-tool export) to confirm the seasonality and underlying trend. Check SERP competition for the head terms, which are likely dominated by app-store pages and established brands. Estimate what a visit is worth to us.
