# Post-interview debrief

Do it within 2 hours of the interview, while details are fresh. Fill every field from the user's memory; mark anything uncertain `(unsure)`. Do not guess what the interviewer thought.

## Template

```
Company / role / round / date:
Interviewers (name, role):
Format and length:

Questions asked (in order, as close to verbatim as the user recalls):
1.
2.

For each question: story or answer used, and a score from the user's view
  (2 = landed, 1 = acceptable, 0 = weak or stumbled), with one line why.

Things I said that I want to correct or add (and how, in the thank-you):
Things I learned about the role, team or company (facts only):
Concerns I now have about the role (map to the criteria sheet):
Signals from the interviewer (stated, not inferred): next step, timeline, who decides:
Questions I did not get to ask:
```

## After the template

1. Update the tracker row: `status` (screening, interviewing or offer, or `rejected` or `withdrawn` if decided), `last_contact_date` (the interview date), `interview_date` cleared or set to the next round, and `next_action` with a date. The usual next action is the thank-you note within 24 hours, then a check-in on the stated timeline plus 2 working days.
2. Draft the thank-you under the outreach rules. Use at most one correction or addition from the debrief.
3. Story updates: any story that scored 0 gets an edit task in the story bank; any new real experience that came up goes to the bank as a new record.
4. Re-run the tracker script so the weekly plan reflects the new state.
5. Pattern check after three debriefs: if the same question scored 0 twice, make it the first prep item for the next interview.
