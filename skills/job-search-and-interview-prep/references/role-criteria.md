# Target-role criteria sheet

Write the sheet once, before collecting roles, and show it to the user for approval. Roles are scored against it; a role is never tracked on gut feel alone.

## Contents

- Sheet structure
- Scoring
- Decision thresholds
- Worked example

## Sheet structure

Ask the user for three lists. Do not propose criteria they did not state, except to prompt ("What would make you refuse an offer regardless of pay?").

| List | What goes in it | How many |
|---|---|---|
| Deal-breakers | Facts that rule a role out: location or on-site days, required clearance or licence the user lacks, pay floor, a sector they refuse, contract type. | 0 to 5 |
| Must-haves | Things the role needs for the user to want it: scope, seniority, tools, team size, growth path. | 3 to 6 |
| Nice-to-haves | Things that would tip a close call: perks, brand, hybrid flexibility. | 2 to 5 |

Also record: target titles and the accepted synonyms (so searches are not limited to one title), the locations or remote rule, the pay floor and its currency and period, and the earliest start date.

## Scoring

Score each must-have and nice-to-have from the job post only, using what the post actually says.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | The post states it clearly. |
| 1 | The post implies it, or it is partly met. |
| 0 | Not mentioned or contradicted. |

Weights: must-have 3, nice-to-have 1. Maximum = 2 x (3 x number of must-haves + number of nice-to-haves). Unknown is 0, not 1: a post that is silent on pay or scope gets no credit, and the gap goes in the "ask the recruiter" column.

## Decision thresholds

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Any deal-breaker present | Do not track. Say which one. |
| Score at least 70% of maximum and no must-have at 0 | Track as `preparing` and apply. |
| Score 50 to 69%, or one must-have at 0 | Track as `wishlist`; list the questions to ask before applying. |
| Under 50% | Do not track. |

These cut-offs are working values chosen to keep the apply list short enough to tailor each application; the user may change them.

## Worked example

Must-haves: hands-on SQL, remote or hybrid within the user's country, team of at least 3 analysts. Nice-to-haves: mentoring, a named learning budget. Maximum = 2 x (3 x 3 + 2) = 22. A post that clearly states SQL (2), says "remote-first in the US" (2), is silent on team size (0), mentions mentoring (2) and no budget (0) scores 3x2 + 3x2 + 3x0 + 2 + 0 = 14, which is 64%, and one must-have is 0: track as `wishlist` and ask the recruiter about team size.
