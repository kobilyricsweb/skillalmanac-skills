Vortiq B7 blender review: six weeks of smoothies and soup

By Maya Okonkwo. Published 14 October 2026, updated 9 November 2026.

Our score: 8.7 out of 10. The B7 crushes ice evenly and is quieter than its rivals, but the lid is fiddly.

Price when tested: $179.00 at the manufacturer's shop.

What other buyers say: 4.8 stars on Amazon (2,314 ratings) at the time of writing.

How we tested

Forty blends over six weeks: frozen fruit, nut butter, hot soup. Noise measured at one metre: 84 dB.

How to clean the B7 in three steps

  1. Half fill with warm water and a drop of soap.
  2. Run on pulse for 30 seconds.
  3. Rinse and leave upside down to dry.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Vortiq B7 dishwasher safe?

The jug and lid are top-rack safe. The base is not.

Can it blend hot soup?

Yes, up to 1.2 litres, with the vented lid cap open.

How long is the warranty?

Seven years on the motor, two on the jug.
