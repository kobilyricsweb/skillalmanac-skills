# Market overview

- The market grew in several segments during the quarter
- Competitors launched new products in three regions
- Pricing pressure continued in the enterprise segment
- Our share held steady in the mid-market segment
- Analysts expect slower growth in the second half
- Currency movements affected reported revenue in Europe
- Supply chain delays eased after the spring disruption
- Hiring plans were paused in two business units
