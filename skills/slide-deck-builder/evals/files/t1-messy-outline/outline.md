# Delivery performance

- On-time delivery was 91% in 2025 and 96% in 2026
- Late deliveries fell from 4,800 to 2,300
- The new routing tool was introduced in March
- Two depots account for most of the improvement
- Customer complaints about delays fell 38%

# Costs

- Cost per parcel fell from 3.10 to 2.85
- Fuel is the biggest single line
- Overtime fell once routes were shorter
