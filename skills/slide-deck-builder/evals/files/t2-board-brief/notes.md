# Raw notes for the board deck

Audience: four board members, all familiar with the business, not with the new product.
Slot: 15 minutes including questions. Live, in a meeting room.
Goal: get approval to spend 120,000 on a pilot of the self-service portal in the northern region.

Facts we have:
- Support calls in the northern region: 9,400 a month today.
- In a two-month trial with 600 customers, calls per customer fell from 1.6 to 1.1 a month.
- Trial data comes from the call centre logs, March and April of this year.
- Portal build cost: 85,000 one-off, plus 35,000 for a year of hosting and content.
- Risk: older customers may not use the portal; 22% of trial customers did not log in at all.

Facts we do not have yet: the saving in money per avoided call, and the expected take-up across the whole region.
