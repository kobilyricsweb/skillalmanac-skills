# Notice of Change to Benefit Payments

In the event that a recipient is in receipt of a payment that exceeds the amount to which they are entitled, the Department will commence recovery proceedings subsequent to the issuance of a written notification. Recipients who are required to submit supplementary documentation must do so within 14 days of the date shown on the notification, and the documentation must be sent to the address below or to claims@benefits.example. Failure to comply may result in the termination of the benefit.

A hearing may be requested by the recipient, and the request must be made in writing no later than 30 days after the decision was issued. Payments of $1,250.50 per month will continue until the hearing has been held, unless fraud is suspected. Questions regarding this notice can be directed to 0800 555 0199 between 9:00 and 17:00 on weekdays.
