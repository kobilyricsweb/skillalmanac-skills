Required fields: approved_samples, explicit_voice_preferences, and prohibited_tone_patterns. Each sample should include its text and, when supplied, its context. Preferences should identify the desired voice or wording behavior. Prohibitions should identify banned patterns and any stated scope. Optional context limits may restrict a rule to a channel, audience, or content type.

If a required field is absent, return a partial card with the missing field named and do not convert observations into rules. If samples conflict with explicit preferences, preserve both and mark the conflict. Output fields are: constraint, type, source, scope, writer test, counterexample if supported, and unresolved question. Distinguish observed patterns from owner-approved constraints. Do not validate factual claims, infer brand promises, normalize terminology, predict audience response, or rewrite the supplied copy unless separately requested. A tone card describes wording boundaries; it does not certify truth or performance.
