# Moving the office

We will move the office to the new building at the end of June. Facilities is handling the move and IT will take care of the network. The new landlord has promised the building will be ready by 20 June, and we have told staff that desks will be ready on the first day. The old lease runs out on 30 June, so there is only a short margin.

Packing starts in the week of 15 June. Everyone packs their own boxes. The movers are booked for the weekend of 27 June. Once the movers are done, IT sets up the network and phones on Monday morning, and staff start work in the new office the same day.
