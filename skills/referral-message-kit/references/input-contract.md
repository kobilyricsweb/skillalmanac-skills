Required fields:

- program_rules: complete supplied referral terms.
- eligibility: inviter and recipient eligibility, including exclusions.
- reward_conditions: required actions, timing, amount or form, clearance or settlement event, and delivery condition.
- expiration: supplied end date or explicit statement that none is provided.
- prohibited_wording: claims or phrases that must not appear.
- approved_cta: supplied action wording.

If a required field is missing, return drafts with the affected claim marked unresolved and list the missing field. Output must contain inviter text, recipient text, short disclosure, ineligible-case response, fact trace, assumptions or conflicts, and scope boundary. Preserve every material condition for receiving the reward. The short disclosure must stand on its own: when supplied, it includes the qualifying action, payment amount, deadline, clearance or settlement condition, reward form, and delivery timing rather than relying on another message to supply them. Do not infer eligibility, instant delivery, guaranteed rewards, or program authorization. Exact amounts and dates must match the supplied rules. A message kit is copy guidance, not an eligibility decision or operational referral action.
