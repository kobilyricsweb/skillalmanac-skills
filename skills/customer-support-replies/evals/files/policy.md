# Brightleaf Home - customer service policy (agent copy)

## Returns and refunds
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item.
- Between 31 and 60 days after delivery, unused items in original packaging can be returned for store credit only.
- After 60 days, and for used items at any time, returns are not accepted. Faulty electrical items are handled under the warranty.
- The original shipping fee is not refunded, except when the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
- The customer pays return shipping, except for damaged or wrong items, where we email a prepaid return label.

## Damaged or wrong items
- The customer must report damage within 7 days of delivery and send a photo.
- The customer chooses a replacement or a refund. We email a prepaid return label once we have the photo.
- A replacement ships when the carrier scans the returned parcel.

## Warranty
- Electrical items carry a 1-year warranty from the delivery date against faults.
- Warranty claims are made through the warranty form at brightleafhome.example/warranty. The warranty team answers within 3 business days.
- Agents do not diagnose faults or promise a warranty outcome.

## Orders and delivery
- An order can be changed or cancelled, and its address changed, only while its status is "Processing". Once the status is "Shipped" we cannot change it.
- Delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees.
- If tracking has not updated for 10 business days, we open a trace with the carrier. A trace takes up to 8 business days. If the trace confirms the parcel is lost, the customer chooses a reshipment or a refund.
- We do not reship or refund a parcel in transit before a trace is complete.

## Discounts, credits and exceptions
- Agents cannot offer discounts, vouchers, compensation or exceptions to this policy.
- A goodwill credit can be approved only by the support supervisor. The agent passes the request on and does not tell the customer what the outcome will be.
- We do not adjust prices after purchase.
