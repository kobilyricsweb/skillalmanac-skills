The old lighthouse stands on the northern cape and has guided ships for more than two hundred years. Its lamp was replaced by an electric beam in the last century, but the keeper's house is still lived in by a family who tend the garden.

Visitors may climb the stair on weekends. The tower is closed in winter, when the wind off the sea makes the gallery unsafe. A small museum at the base shows photographs of the harbour and the ships that once called there.
