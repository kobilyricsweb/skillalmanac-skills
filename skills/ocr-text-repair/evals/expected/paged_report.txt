Revenue grew in every region this year, led by the northern division, which added 14 new customers and kept every existing contract. The board approved a budget of 2,400,000 for the coming year.
[[FF]]
The southern division had a harder year. Two plants closed and 310 staff were moved to other sites, while the existing sales team was cut by a third in the second half of the year.
[[FF]]
Costs fell by 6 percent after the closures, and the board expects a further saving of 1.5 million next year if the plan holds and no new plant needs to be closed.
[[FF]]
The outlook for the coming year is steady. Orders are up and the order book already covers 8 months of production in the northern division and 5 months in the southern one.
