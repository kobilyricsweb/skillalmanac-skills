It covers the problem (18% no-shows), the v1 scope (SMS only, confirm/cancel link, instant slot release on cancel, per-salon off switch, dated opt-in as a legal requirement), and what is deferred (WhatsApp).

Left open because the notes do not settle them:
1. **24 vs 48 hours.** Salons want 48 and Noor wants 24. Sending both doubles the cost.
2. **Who pays for SMS.** Worth settling before launch, since it affects pricing.
3. **A no-show target.** Nobody set one. The baseline is 18%.

Two things to watch: only about 70% of clients have a mobile number, and the opt-in requirement may shrink that further, so reach caps the improvement. Also, the notes do not say how opt-in is collected, so I listed it as a question for design and legal.
