appointment reminders - raw notes

context: our booking app is used by hair and beauty salons. owners keep asking for reminders.

numbers we have
- no-show rate across all salons last quarter: 18% of appointments (from the data team, Noor)
- SMS costs us $0.012 per message with the current provider
- about 70% of clients have a mobile number on file, the rest booked by phone and the salon never entered it

what was said in the planning meeting
- Felix (PM): send a text reminder before the appointment with a link to confirm or cancel
- Gwen (salon success): salons want 48 hours before, so they have time to fill a cancelled slot
- Noor: the studies she has seen use 24 hours. she thinks 48 is too early and people forget again
- no decision on 24 vs 48. someone said "why not both" and Felix said that doubles the SMS cost
- legal (Idris): we must have the client's opt-in before texting them. opt-in has to be recorded with a date. not optional.
- Felix wants WhatsApp too. Pavel (eng): SMS only in the first version, WhatsApp needs a business account approval we don't have
- Pavel: cancel link should free the slot in the calendar right away
- Gwen: salons must be able to switch reminders off for their salon, some send their own
- who pays for the SMS? us or the salon? Felix: "figure out later"
- nobody set a target for the no-show rate
