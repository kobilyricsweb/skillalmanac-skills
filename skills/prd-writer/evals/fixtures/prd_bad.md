# PRD: Saved carts

## Problem
Users lose their carts. 23% of mobile sessions abandon the cart, costing an estimated $250,000 a year.

## Goals
- Make carts easy to find again
- Increase conversion by 15% by December 15

## Requirements
- R1 Carts should sync quickly and seamlessly across devices.
- **R2 (Must)** Saved carts are kept for 90 days.
- The UI should be intuitive.
