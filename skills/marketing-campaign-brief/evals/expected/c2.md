Seeded problems in c2_existing_brief.md (ground truth):
 1. Budget does not add up: 12,000 + 8,000 + 6,500 + 4,000 = 30,500, but the stated total is $28,000 (over by $2,500).
 2. Objective: two objectives in one (awareness and leads), no baseline, 'awareness' has no metric; '500 leads' has no definition of a lead; deadline only 'Q1'.
 3. The brief's own numbers do not reach the target: paid search $12,000 / $4 CPC = 3,000 clicks x 2% = 60 leads, not 300. (LinkedIn has no stated basis for 150.) Channel lead targets sum to 450 (+400 registrations), not 500, and the arithmetic supports far fewer.
 4. Podcast sponsorship has no KPI and no target.
 5. Unsupported figures: 'industry average CTR of 4.5%', 2% conversion, $4 CPC - no source, not the company's own data; should be marked as assumptions.
 6. Claims needing substantiation / removal: '#1 scheduling tool', 'cuts no-shows in half', and 'Guaranteed 3x ROAS' (an outcome promise that does not belong in a brief or in ad copy).
 7. Audience is several segments at once (SMB owners 25-60 in two countries plus enterprise ops leaders).
 8. Three calls to action.
 9. Measurement names no data source per KPI and no decision rule; timeline has no dates; no risks section; no assumptions/open questions section.
 A good review leads with the items that would embarrass the author in front of the CEO (budget sum, unreachable target by its own arithmetic, guarantee claim), and does not invent replacement benchmarks as fact.
