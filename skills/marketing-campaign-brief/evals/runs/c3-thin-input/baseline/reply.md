# Reply: first draft of the spring promo brief

The draft is in brief.md. It is a first pass built on assumptions, because the request gave only the bakery, the season, and the $1,500 budget. Nothing was checked against real data, and no web research was done.

## What I assumed (please correct)

- A limited-time spring offer (seasonal item or bundle) with a 3 to 4 week window.
- Local geo-targeted social ads plus Google Business Profile, with a small email/SMS push to existing customers.
- Budget split: $600 paid social, $300 Google local, $100 email/SMS, $350 in-store print, $150 contingency.
- Audience within roughly 5 to 10 km of the shop.

## What I need from you to finalize

1. Shop name, location or neighborhood, and logo or brand colors.
2. The actual offer: product(s), price or discount, and dates. Is it in stock and staffed?
3. Campaign start and end dates.
4. Your goal and what success looks like (e.g., extra sales, number of redemptions, cost per result).
5. Do you already have a social account, Google Business Profile, and an email or SMS list? How big is the list?
6. Who runs the ads and content: you, a freelancer, or an agency?
7. Any photos or videos you already have, and whether spring products are already on the menu.
8. Whether the $1,500 is all ad spend or includes printing and staff time, and how much is already committed.
9. Any past promos and their results, so I can set realistic expectations.

Once I have these, I can tighten the offer, timing, and budget split, and write the actual ad copy.
