# Spring Promo Campaign Brief (First Draft)

Status: DRAFT v0.1. Built on assumptions, marked [ASSUMPTION]. Items marked [TBD] need owner input before launch.

## 1. Summary

A short spring promotion for the bakery, run mainly through local paid social and Google Business Profile, with an in-store and email push to existing customers. Total budget: $1,500.

## 2. Objective

- Primary: drive in-store visits and spring product sales during the promo window.
- Secondary: grow the email or contact list and get reviews or follow-ups from new visitors.
- Target result [TBD]: for example, X extra transactions or $Y in promo-item revenue over the window.

## 3. Offer [TBD]

[ASSUMPTION] A spring-themed limited-time offer, such as a seasonal pastry or cake bundle, or a "buy 2, get 1" style deal on spring items. Final offer, price, and any exclusions must be confirmed by the owner.

## 4. Timing [TBD]

- [ASSUMPTION] 3 to 4 weeks, starting late March or April (adjust to the actual spring window).
- Pre-launch teaser: 1 week before.
- Peak push: opening weekend and the two weekends after.
- Close-out: final 3 days, "last chance" messaging.

## 5. Audience

- Primary: people living or working within about 5 to 10 km (3 to 6 miles) of the shop, aged roughly 25 to 55, who buy treats or gifts.
- Secondary: existing customers and email subscribers.
- [TBD] Confirm radius, age range, and whether the shop serves a specific neighborhood or commuter area.

## 6. Key Message

"Spring is here, and so is [offer name] at [shop name]. Fresh, limited, and made today."

Supporting points [TBD]: freshly baked daily, local ingredients (if true), the offer dates, and where to find the shop.

## 7. Channels and Budget Split ($1,500)

| Channel | Budget | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Local paid social (Meta/Instagram, geo-targeted) | $600 | Reach nearby adults, with 2 to 3 photo or short-video ads |
| Google Business Profile posts and local search ads | $300 | Capture "bakery near me" intent; post weekly updates |
| Email and SMS to existing customers | $100 | Tools and send costs; reactivate past buyers |
| In-store signage, flyers, and table tents | $350 | Printing and POS cards for walk-in traffic |
| Contingency and boosted posts | $150 | Extend the best-performing ad or post |

[ASSUMPTION] These are planning figures, not quotes. Adjust once actual costs are known.

## 8. Creative Needs

- 3 hero product photos (spring items), in natural light.
- 2 short videos (10 to 15 seconds) of baking or the display case.
- Ad copy in 3 variants (short, friendly, offer-led).
- 1 A-frame or window sign and 1 counter card.
- Logo and brand colors [TBD: provide files].

## 9. Measurement

- Tracked by: unique promo code or "mention this ad" offer, Google Business Profile insights (calls, direction requests, website clicks), and POS sales for the promo items.
- Weekly check: spend, reach, clicks, promo redemptions, and revenue vs. the previous 4 weeks.
- Success threshold [TBD]: owner to define (for example, cost per redemption under $X).

## 10. Roles and Approvals [TBD]

- Owner: final sign-off on offer, pricing, and copy.
- Ad setup and management: [TBD] (self, freelancer, or agency).
- Content production: [TBD].

## 11. Risks and Notes

- Budget is small. Paid social works best with a tight radius and a clear offer; expect modest reach.
- Offer must be confirmed in stock and staffed before launch.
- Check local rules for promotional pricing and for any sign or sidewalk display permits.
- No competitor, past-performance, or market data was provided; none is used here.
