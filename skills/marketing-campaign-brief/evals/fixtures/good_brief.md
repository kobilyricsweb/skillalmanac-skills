# Campaign brief: Ledgerly recurring invoices launch

## Objective
Primary objective: trial signups from 310 a month [given] to 450 a month [assumption] by 2027-03-17.
Why now: the recurring-invoices feature ships on 2027-02-03.

## Audience
Freelancers who invoice the same clients every month and still copy last month's invoice by hand.
Not targeting: agencies with a finance team.

## Offer and message
Offer: 30-day free trial with recurring invoices included.
Message: Set the invoice once; it sends itself.
Proof: feature demo. The claim about faster payment is not used until data exists.
Call to action: Start a free trial.

## Channel plan
| Channel | Role | Budget | KPI | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search ads | Capture demand | $9,000 [given] | Trial signups | 150 [calc] | Marketer |
| Email to list | Convert | $1,000 [assumption] | Trial signups | 60 [assumption] | Marketer |
| Creator test | Create demand | $6,000 [assumption] | Visits from new users | 3,000 [assumption] | Marketer |
| Reserve | Move to what works | $2,000 [calc] | - | - | Marketer |

## Budget
Total budget: $18,000 [given]

## Measurement
| KPI | Data source | Checked | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial signups | Product analytics, UTM tagged | Weekly | If search cost per signup is above $60 [assumption] after 2 weeks, pause the weakest ad groups and move money to email |

Search path: $9,000 [given] / $3.10 [given] = 2,903 clicks [calc]; x 5.2% [given] = 151 signups [calc].

## Timeline
Assets ready 2027-01-27. Launch 2027-02-03. First review 2027-02-17. End 2027-03-17.

## Risks and guardrails
- The "paid faster" claim has no data: not used.
- Creator channel is new to the team: treated as a test.

## Assumptions and open questions
- Target of 450 signups a month.
- Email production cost of $1,000.
- Creator test budget and its 3,000-visit target.
- Budget is $18,000 or $20,000? The notes give both.
