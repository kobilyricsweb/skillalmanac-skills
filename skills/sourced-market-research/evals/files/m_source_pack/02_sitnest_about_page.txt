About SitNest
Founded in Toronto in 2021. SitNest connects pet parents with vetted local sitters and dog walkers.
Trusted by 90,000 pet parents since launch.
How we make money: sitters keep 82% of each booking; SitNest retains 18%.
Available in Ontario, Quebec and British Columbia.
