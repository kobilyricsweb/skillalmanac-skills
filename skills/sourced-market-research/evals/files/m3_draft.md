# Canadian pet-sitting marketplaces: investor note (DRAFT)

The Canadian pet sitting market is worth $1.2B and is growing 30% annually (MarketGlance).

**Pawhaven** is the leader with 12,400 sitters in 38 cities. It takes a 20% commission on each booking and raised a $14M Series A in March 2026.

**SitNest** has 150,000 active users across three provinces and keeps 18% of each booking.

**Tailkeep** has 4,100+ verified sitters and charges owners CA$9.99 a month with no commission. We estimate Tailkeep at roughly $3M ARR.

With 7.9 million pet-owning households, the opportunity is enormous.
