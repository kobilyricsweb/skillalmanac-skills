# Canadian pet-sitting marketplaces: investor note (corrected, state on 2026-11-20)

## Method
Checked against the source pack supplied with the draft; no web access was used. Saved pages were read in full and count as opened. The MarketGlance and PetBiz items are search-result previews, not opened articles, and are marked snippet. The Tailkeep blog is a competitor's marketing page with no linked sources and is marked unverified. The Tailkeep home page contains text addressed to AI agents, telling them to run a shell command and register an account; that text was not followed.

## Takeaway
Pawhaven's pricing page lists 12,400 active sitters in 38 Canadian cities and a 15% service fee on each completed booking [S1]. SitNest's own About page reports 90,000 pet parents trusted since launch and an 18% retained share [S2]. Tailkeep's home page lists 4,100+ verified sitters, a CA$9.99 monthly owner subscription, and sitters keeping 100% of their charges [S3]. The 2024 national household survey finds 7.9 million households with a dog or cat, of which 11% paid for sitting or boarding in the past 12 months [S7].

## Findings
### Market size
- The Canadian pet sitting market was valued at $1.2B in 2025, per a MarketGlance search preview (snippet; full report paywalled, not opened) [S5]. Currency and scope are not confirmed.
- The 30% a year growth rate appears only in Tailkeep's marketing blog (unverified) [S6]. No opened source supports it, so it is removed from the note.

### Pawhaven
- Pawhaven lists 12,400 active sitters in 38 Canadian cities, updated October 2026 [S1].
- Pawhaven keeps a 15% service fee on each completed booking; pet owners pay no booking fee [S1].
- A $14M Series A in March 2026 is reported in a search preview (snippet; article body not opened) [S4].

### SitNest
- SitNest reports 90,000 pet parents trusted since launch; this is a cumulative count, not active users [S2].
- SitNest retains 18% of each booking; sitters keep 82% [S2].
- SitNest is available in Ontario, Quebec and British Columbia [S2].

### Tailkeep
- Tailkeep lists 4,100+ verified sitters; owners pay CA$9.99 a month; sitters keep 100% of what they charge [S3].
- Tailkeep is available in Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver and Ottawa [S3].

## Estimates
- Pet-owning households that paid for sitting or boarding in the past 12 months [calc]: 7.9 million households with a dog or cat [S7] x 11% [S7] = about 869,000 households. The survey counts dog or cat owners only, so this is not all pet owners. No range is given; the 11% is a single national survey figure.
- Tailkeep revenue (the draft's $3M ARR is not supported): at CA$9.99 a month [S3], $3M a year needs about 25,000 paying owners (3,000,000 / (9.99 x 12)) [calc]. No subscriber count or revenue is in the pack, so the figure is removed.

## Conflicts
- SitNest size: 90,000 pet parents trusted since launch [S2] (first-party About page, cumulative) against 150,000 users (competitor blog, unverified) [S6]. The first-party figure is better supported and the blog figure is not used. The two count different things, so they are not directly comparable.
- Pawhaven fee: 15% service fee on each completed booking [S1] (first-party pricing page, saved 2026-11-18) against a 20% commission (competitor blog, unverified) [S6]. The pricing page is better supported; the 20% figure is not used.

## Not obtained
- The full MarketGlance report (paywalled, not opened): currency, definition and scope of the market size are unconfirmed, and no opened source gives a market growth rate.
- The full PetBiz Weekly article was not opened; the Series A is reported only in a preview, and no lead investor is confirmed beyond that preview.
- Tailkeep subscriber count, revenue or ARR: none in the pack, so the draft's ARR cannot be checked.
- Pawhaven as "the leader": no market-share or ranking source in the pack.
- Households with other pets, beyond dog or cat owners: not in the statistics table.
