# Canada pet sitting market size: answer for the slide

**No dollar market size is supported by an opened source.** The pack has no published CA$ figure for Canadian pet sitting that anyone opened. Do not put a dollar number on the slide from this pack.

**Best-supported figure (use this):** about 869,000 Canadian households paid for pet sitting or boarding at least once in the past 12 months. [calc: 7.9 million households with a dog or cat x 11% paid for sitting or boarding] [S6] [estimate]

- Inputs: 7.9 million pet-owning households (Table 3, [S6]) and 11% (Table 5, [S6]), from a national survey of 21,000 households fielded March-May 2024.
- This is a household count, not a dollar value. The pack has no average spend per booking, so no dollar figure can be derived from it. [S6]
- Caveat: the 11% covers sitting and boarding together, so the count is not sitting alone. [S6]

**Weaker figure, not for the slide:** a 2025 Canadian pet sitting market value of $1.2B. [S4] (snippet) It appears only in a search preview of a paywalled report (US$3,950). The article was not opened, so the figure is unverified and its definition is unknown.
