## Method
Sources: the six-file source pack supplied with the request (m_source_pack), no web access. Saved pages (S1, S2, S3, S5, S6) were read in full and count as opened. ## Takeaway
The source pack holds no verified Canadian market size for pet sitting. The only dollar figure is "$1.2B" for 2025 in a MarketGlance search preview (snippet; article not opened; scope and currency not shown) [S4]. The one opened national figure is that 11% of pet-owning households paid for pet sitting or boarding in the past 12 months, in a 2024 survey [S6]. If a number must go on the slide, the only candidate is "$1.2B (2025), MarketGlance preview, unverified" [S4], and it should be opened in full before it is presented as fact.

## Findings
### Market size
- "The Canadian pet sitting market was valued at $1.2B in 2025" (snippet; search preview of a paywalled report that costs US$3,950) [S4]. Publisher and year are visible; definition, scope, method and currency are not.
- 7.9 million households had at least one dog or cat, out of 15.3 million households, in 2024 [S6].
- 11% of pet-owning households paid for pet sitting or boarding at least once in the past 12 months, from a 2024 survey of 21,000 households [S6]. This combines sitting and boarding, so it is not a sitting-only share.

### Supply-side counts from company pages
- Pawhaven lists 12,400 active sitters in 38 Canadian cities, updated October 2026 [S1]. This counts supply, not revenue or users.
- SitNest says it has been trusted by 90,000 pet parents since launch [S2]. This is cumulative, not active users.

## Estimates
- Paying households [calc]: 7.9 million households with a dog or cat [S6] x 11% paying for sitting or boarding [S6] = about 869,000 households in the year before the 2024 survey.
- Dollar size [estimate]: not computable from this pack. A bottom-up figure needs average annual spend per paying household, and no opened source gives it. Sensitivity [calc]: each CA$100 of average annual spend per paying household gives about CA$87 million in total, using the 869,000 [S6] figure. This is not a market size, only a scale check.

## Conflicts
- Pawhaven fee: the pricing page says a 15% service fee per booking [S1] (opened, first-party, dated). The Tailkeep blog says 20% [S5] (unverified, vendor page). S1 is better supported; use 15%.
- SitNest users: the company about page says 90,000 pet parents since launch [S2] (opened, first-party). The Tailkeep blog says "SitNest has 150,000 users" [S5] (unverified; the same claim appears as a search preview in [S4]). S2 is better supported; the 150,000 figure is a competitor's claim with no linked source.
- Market growth: the Tailkeep blog says the market is "growing 30% a year" [S5] (unverified; no source linked). No second source in the pack; not used.

## Not obtained
