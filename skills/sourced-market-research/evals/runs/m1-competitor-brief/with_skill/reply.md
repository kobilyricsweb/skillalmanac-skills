# Canadian pet-sitting marketplaces: competitor brief (state on 2026-11-20)

## Method
Sources come only from the source pack (m_source_pack); no web access was used. Saved pages were read in full, and their saved dates (2026-11-18) are given in the ledger. "Opened" means the saved page text was read. The search-result file holds previews only, so those items are snippet. The Tailkeep blog is written by a competitor and links no sources, so its claims are marked unverified. The Tailkeep home page contains text addressed to AI assistants, asking the reader to run a shell script and register an account before reading further. That text was not followed. The pack holds no figures for the company that commissioned this brief, so no positioning against the competitors is made.

## Takeaway
Three Canadian marketplaces are documented on opened pages. Pawhaven states 12,400 active sitters in 38 cities [S1], while Tailkeep states 4,100+ verified sitters [S3]; the two counts are defined differently and are not comparable. SitNest keeps 18% of each booking [S2], Pawhaven keeps a 15% service fee [S1], and Tailkeep charges owners CA$9.99 a month with sitters keeping 100% [S3]. No published dollar market size was opened: the only one is a paywalled preview (snippet) [S5]. The best-supported size figure is about 869,000 Canadian pet-owning households that paid for sitting or boarding in the 12 months before the 2024 survey [S8] [calc].

## Findings
### Who operates and where
- Pawhaven: 12,400 active sitters in 38 Canadian cities, as updated in October 2026 [S1].
- SitNest: founded in Toronto in 2021; connects pet parents with vetted local sitters and dog walkers; available in Ontario, Quebec and British Columbia [S2].
- Tailkeep: 4,100+ verified sitters; now in Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver and Ottawa [S3].
- Pawhaven is reported to have raised a $14M Series A in March 2026, led by Northlake Ventures (snippet; article body not opened) [S4].

### What each charges
- Pawhaven: sitter profile is free; Pawhaven keeps a 15% service fee on each completed booking; pet owners pay no booking fee; optional featured profile at CA$19 per month. Payouts within 2 business days; the Pawhaven Guarantee covers vet costs up to CA$5,000 [S1]. A sitter therefore keeps 85% of each booking [S1] [calc].
- SitNest: sitters keep 82% of each booking; SitNest retains 18% [S2].
- Tailkeep: owners subscribe at CA$9.99 a month and book as often as they like; sitters keep 100% of what they charge [S3].

### Size signals as published
- SitNest: "Trusted by 90,000 pet parents since launch", a company claim with no date or definition of the count [S2].
- Pawhaven: 12,400 active sitters; no owner count is published [S1].
- Tailkeep: 4,100+ verified sitters; no active-sitter or owner count is published [S3].
- Tailkeep's blog gives SitNest "150,000 users" (unverified) [S7]; the same figure appears in a search preview [S6] (snippet).

### Market size
- Published: a market report preview puts the Canadian pet sitting market at $1.2B for 2025 and sells the full report for US$3,950 (snippet; preview only, article not opened; the preview does not show the currency or definition behind the $1.2B) [S5]. It is not verified and is not used in the estimate below.
- Bottom-up, in households: 7.9 million Canadian households have at least one dog or cat, out of 15.3 million households [S8]; 11% of pet-owning households paid for sitting or boarding at least once in the past 12 months [S8]. See Estimates.

### Claims to treat with care
- Tailkeep's blog says the market is growing 30% a year and that commission-free is "the future" (unverified; no source linked) [S7]. Not used in this brief.

## Estimates
- Canadian pet-owning households that paid for sitting or boarding in the 12 months before the 2024 survey: 7.9 million households with a dog or cat [S8] x 11% that paid for sitting or boarding [S8] = about 869,000 households [estimate]. The 11% is one survey share with no range published alongside it, so no low or high case is given. The count includes boarding as well as sitting, and it is based on self-reported use.
- A dollar size is not estimated: it would need average spend per household per year and visits per year, and neither is in the pack.

## Conflicts
- Pawhaven fee: 15% service fee on each completed booking [S1], from Pawhaven's own pricing page, against 20% [S7], from Tailkeep's blog (unverified). This brief uses S1: it is first-party, and the blog is a competitor's comparison that links no source.
- SitNest users: "90,000 pet parents since launch" [S2], from SitNest's about page (undated, cumulative), against "150,000 users" [S7], from Tailkeep's blog (unverified; also in preview [S6], snippet). This brief uses S2 as the first-party figure, but the two may count different things and the blog gives no date or method.
- Sitter counts: 12,400 active sitters [S1] against 4,100+ verified sitters [S3]. These count different things and are not comparable; do not rank the two by them.

## Not obtained
- A dollar market size for Canadian pet sitting with a stated definition, currency and year. The MarketGlance full report is paywalled; only the preview [S5] was seen.
- Booking volume, average booking value, or gross booking value for any of the three marketplaces.
- Owner (pet-parent) counts for Pawhaven and Tailkeep; a sitter count for SitNest.
- The Pawhaven Series A amount and lead investor: only the search preview was seen, and the article was not opened [S4].
- Tailkeep blog's 30% annual growth claim: no source linked; not used [S7].
- SitNest owner pricing: no pricing page is in the pack.
- Active-user and retention figures for any competitor.
- Any household survey after 2024: the bureau table is the 2024 survey [S8].
