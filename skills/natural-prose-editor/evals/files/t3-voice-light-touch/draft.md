I never trusted the ferry schedule. Nobody did. It said 7:40, the boat left when Old Gus felt like it, and that was fine by us.

It is worth noting that the crossing took 25 minutes in good weather. Bad weather, an hour, sometimes two. My mother kept a thermos of coffee in the glove box for exactly that.

We stopped caring about the schedule the winter the bridge to Marrow Point was announced. Then it was never built, and the ferry stayed.
