# Quarterly update

Revenue grew 14% in Q3 and reached $2.3 million. Most of the growth came from renewals.

We built a retention dashboard for Maria Lopez and shipped it in October. Forty users tested it first, and their notes changed the layout twice. Two of them asked for CSV export, which is still open.

The next quarter is mostly about support costs, which rose faster than revenue. If they keep climbing, we will pause the Berlin hiring plan until the numbers settle.
