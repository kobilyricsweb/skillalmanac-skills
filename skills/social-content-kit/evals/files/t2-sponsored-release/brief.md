# Sponsor brief: Northwind Ledger Sync 2.0

Northwind is paying Harbor Ledger a flat fee for two posts about the Ledger Sync 2.0 release on 1 December 2026. Posts go on LinkedIn and Instagram.

Facts Northwind approved for use:
- Ledger Sync 2.0 releases on 1 December 2026.
- It syncs ledgers every 15 minutes instead of nightly.
- Pricing starts at 29 USD per user per month.
- Link: https://northwind.example/sync2

Northwind asks that posts do not claim time savings, because it has no data on them. Harbor Ledger's contract requires the posts to be marked as paid.
