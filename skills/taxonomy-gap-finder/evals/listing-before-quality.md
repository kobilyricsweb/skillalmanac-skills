# taxonomy-gap-finder

Unpublished original review draft. Editorial tier: free. No Platform Score or public sale.

## One Line

Finds assignment and definition gaps in a supplied taxonomy snapshot.

## Audience

Taxonomy maintainers reviewing coverage and category clarity.

## Deliverable

Coverage report for unassigned items, conflicts, weak definitions, and questions.

## Limits

No graph validation, invented assignments, keyword ranking, authority claims, or external facts.
