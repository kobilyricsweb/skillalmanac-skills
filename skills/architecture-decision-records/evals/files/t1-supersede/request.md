Last week the team agreed to move order events from RabbitMQ to the shared Kafka cluster that the platform team already runs for analytics. Reasons from the meeting: four consumers now want order events, two of them must rebuild state from history, and RabbitMQ cannot replay. Staying on RabbitMQ would cost about two engineer days per backfill. Moving costs about six weeks of producer and consumer rewrites, and ordering becomes per partition instead of per queue. Nobody objected. Please record this in the adr folder.
