# Northfield Outdoor refund policy (internal, version 3)

- Unworn, unwashed items can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Items marked Final Sale cannot be returned.
- Return shipping is free for orders over 60 dollars; otherwise a flat 6 dollar label fee is deducted from the refund.
- Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the warehouse scans the parcel.
- Gift recipients receive store credit, not cash, unless the buyer is the one returning the item.
- Damaged or defective items can be returned at any time within 12 months, with free shipping and a choice of refund or replacement.
- Tents and sleeping bags may be returned once opened if they were not used outdoors.
- The policy does not cover price matching, warranty repairs, or orders placed in physical stores; those are handled by other teams.
