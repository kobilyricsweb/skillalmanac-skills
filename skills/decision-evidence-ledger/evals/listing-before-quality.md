# decision-evidence-ledger

Status: unpublished free candidate; no score or verification badge.

## One Line

Separate supplied decision notes into decisions, proposals, assumptions, conflicts, and evidence.

## Audience

Project leads, analysts, reviewers, and handoff owners.

## Benefit

Creates traceable decision state without silently resolving uncertainty.

## Limits

Works only from supplied notes; it does not interview, decide, or create minutes.

## Suggested Example

Fictional example: reconcile a short product note where two dated approvals disagree.
