## Accessibility audit: product.html (Atelier Dumont canvas tote)
Standard: WCAG 2.2 level AA. Method: static checker (run, 13 FAIL / 14 WARN, every finding opened and confirmed or dropped below) and manual source review in the fixed order. Line numbers refer to product.html. No rendered page, no assistive technology, no browser run.
This is not a conformance claim.

### Findings

Ordered by severity, then by line number. "Found by" is `checker` (script output confirmed in source), `manual` (source review), or `checker + manual`.

### Checked, no failure found
- 1.4.4 Resize text: viewport meta does not block zoom (`user-scalable` absent, no `maximum-scale`).
- 2.4.2 Page titled: `<title>` names the product and the shop.
- 3.1.1 Language of page: `<html lang="en">`.
- 2.4.3 Focus order: DOM order matches visual order, and the buy bar is last in both.
- 2.1.2 No keyboard trap: no focus-trapping widget in the source.
- 2.5.3 Label in name: "Add to cart" and the link text contain their visible text.
- 3.3.2 Labels: the visible "Quantity" label is tied to the input with `for="qty"`.
- 1.4.3 Body text: `#1c1c1c` on white is about 16:1. `.new` `#c0271b` on white is about 5.9:1.
- 1.4.11 Input border `#777` on white is about 4.5:1, which passes 3:1. Icon-button border passes the same way.
- 2.5.8 Swatches (32 px), Add to cart and inline text links are at or above the minimum, or exempt as inline.
- 1.3.4 Orientation: no orientation lock.
- 2.2.1 Timing: no session or timed interaction.
- 2.3.1 Flashing: no flashing content found.
- 3.2.1 and 3.2.2 On focus and On input: no context change from focus or input.
- 3.2.6, 3.3.7, 3.3.8: no help links, no repeated entry, no authentication step on the page.
- 1.3.5 Identify input purpose: no personal-data fields on the page.

- Captions, transcript and video content (item 10): the video file was not available.
- The size-calculator iframe content (item 27).
- Behaviour of drag in Firefox (item 14).

The checker missed the carousel auto-advance (item 11), the 10 px dots (item 3), the stars image (item 21), the price meaning (item 19), the wishlist keyboard gap beyond its drag warning (item 14), and the reflow clipping (item 15).
