Required fields: source excerpts; metadata including dates or versions when supplied; authority and precedence rules; affected artifacts; and the named resolving role. Include source identifiers and enough surrounding context to test whether statements truly conflict. If a required field is missing, identify the gap, preserve the statements as provisional, and avoid a winner declaration. Output fields: conflict summary; exact statements; scope comparison; authority analysis; resolver; precedence result or unresolved status; downstream artifact inventory; decision questions; proposed post-decision actions; and verification notes. The resolver field must preserve the supplied identity or explicitly record that it is missing. Use supplied precedence only; job titles do not establish permission unless the input says so. A source can be controlling for one scope without controlling another. The dossier supports an authorized decision and does not itself change documents or policy.
