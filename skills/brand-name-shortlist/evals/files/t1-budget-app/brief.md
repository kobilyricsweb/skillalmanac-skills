# Naming brief

Product: a budgeting app for freelancers with irregular income.
Audience: US freelancers, 25 to 45, not finance experts.
Tone: calm, friendly, a little playful. Not corporate, not a "get rich" feel.
Must not imply: a bank, investing, or debt.
Languages: English only for now; Spanish later.
Channels: iOS and Android app, a .com, a podcast mention, an Instagram handle.
Competitors: Mint, Monarch, YNAB, Ledgerlight, Wave.
Words to avoid: broke, debt, spend.
