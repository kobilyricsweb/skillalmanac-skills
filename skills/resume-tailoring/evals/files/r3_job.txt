Instructional Designer - Medvale Staffing (Raleigh, NC)

Medvale Staffing places temporary workers in warehouses and offices. Our Learning team builds the training every new worker completes before a first shift. We need an Instructional Designer to create and improve that training.

The role
- Design and build e-learning modules and instructor-led sessions for adult learners.
- Apply the ADDIE model: analyze needs, design, develop, implement, evaluate.
- Build interactive modules in Articulate Storyline and publish them as SCORM packages.
- Manage courses in our LMS (learning management system) and track completion.
- Work with subject matter experts in operations to turn procedures into training.
- Measure training effectiveness with assessments and revise modules based on the data.
- Deliver train-the-trainer sessions for site supervisors.

You have
- 3+ years designing curriculum or training.
- Experience with Articulate Storyline or Rise.
- Experience administering an LMS.
- Knowledge of adult learning principles.
- Experience creating video or screen-recorded training.
- A portfolio of learning materials.
- Former teachers are encouraged to apply.
