Financial Analyst, FP&A - Brightwater Foods (Austin, TX, hybrid)

Brightwater Foods is a regional grocery distributor. The FP&A team supports budgeting, forecasting and monthly reporting for six business units.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain forecasting models for revenue and operating expenses.
- Prepare the monthly variance analysis (actuals vs budget) and explain the drivers to business unit leaders.
- Support the annual budget process.
- Build dashboards in Power BI for sales and margin reporting.
- Pull data with SQL from the finance data warehouse.
- Partner with accounting during month-end close.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in accounting or finance.
- 2-4 years of experience in FP&A, accounting or financial analysis.
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, lookups, modeling).
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau.
- SQL experience.
- CPA or progress toward CPA preferred.
- Experience with an ERP such as NetSuite or SAP is a plus.
- Able to present findings to non-finance managers.
