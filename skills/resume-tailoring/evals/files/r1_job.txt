Customer Success Associate - Tallyroo (remote, US)

Tallyroo makes scheduling software for small retailers. We are hiring a Customer Success Associate to help new customers get set up and keep existing customers happy.

What you will do
- Run customer onboarding for new accounts: kickoff call, setup, training sessions.
- Own a book of about 80 small-business accounts and be their first point of contact.
- Handle escalations and work with support to resolve issues quickly.
- Track account health and renewals in Salesforce; log every customer conversation.
- Answer tickets in Zendesk when the support queue is busy.
- Spot churn risk early and run retention outreach.
- Report on NPS and renewal rates each month.

What we are looking for
- 2+ years in a customer-facing role; retail experience is a plus because our customers are retailers.
- Experience training or onboarding people on software.
- Comfortable with Salesforce or another CRM.
- Zendesk experience preferred.
- Basic SQL for pulling account reports is a plus.
- Clear writing and a calm approach to escalations.
- Spanish is a plus.

Salary range: $52,000 - $60,000.
Tallyroo is an equal opportunity employer.
