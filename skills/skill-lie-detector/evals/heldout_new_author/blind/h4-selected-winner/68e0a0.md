**Why**

- **Multiple comparisons.** If none of the 20 skills helps, the chance that at least one reaches p ≤ 0.05 is 1 − 0.95²⁰ ≈ 64%. The other 19 were not significant, which is about what chance alone predicts. Under a Bonferroni correction the cutoff is 0.05 / 20 = 0.0025, and 0.03 is about 12 times that. Holm's and Benjamini-Hochberg's first step also fail at 0.03.
- **The estimate is likely inflated.** The top result of 20 noisy measurements tends to overstate the true effect (the winner's curse), so the real gain is probably smaller than +7.
