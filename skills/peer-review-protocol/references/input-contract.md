Required fields:
- Assignment prompt and submission format.
- Rubric or supplied assessment criteria.
- Total review duration.
- Allowed feedback scope.

Optional fields may define class roles, anonymity, or required response fields. If the rubric, duration, format, or feedback scope is missing, identify the gap and provide only a provisional protocol. If duration conflicts with required checks, show the arithmetic and flag the conflict rather than silently overloading the schedule.

Output fields:
- Timed review sequence, including a reserved or conditional timed disagreement path.
- Artifact-only checks.
- Evidence stems and locations.
- Author questions.
- Revision choice.
- Disagreement procedure and completion check.

Reviewers distinguish observations from suggestions. The protocol must not assess personal qualities or add criteria not supplied by the assignment or rubric.
