# promotion-variant-check

Status: unpublished free candidate; no score or verification badge.

## One Line

Check promotion message variants against an approved baseline and a declared one-field experiment.

## Audience

Campaign reviewers, release managers, and content QA teams.

## Benefit

Surfaces unchanged variants, multi-field edits, and edits outside the configured field.

## Limits

Static exact comparison only; no copywriting, market research, significance, causality, publishing, or performance claims.

## Synthetic Example

A baseline with headline and body plus changed_field headline accepts a variant changing only headline.
