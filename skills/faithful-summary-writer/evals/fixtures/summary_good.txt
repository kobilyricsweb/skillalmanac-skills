Harbor Library tested weekday closing at 9 pm instead of 6 pm at its main branch for 16 weeks, at a cost of $48,200. Weekday visits rose 18 percent against the same weeks in 2025, and 71 percent of evening visitors had not visited before 6 pm in the previous year. Staffing was the main problem: three part-time employees left, and overtime made up $9,100 of the cost. The county auditor noted that the pilot did not measure retention, so whether new visitors keep coming is unknown. Daniel Reyes, a board member, said the figures suggest the hours may be worth keeping but opposed making them permanent without a staffing plan. The board will vote in November 2026 on extending the hours through the 2027 budget year.
