The Harbor Library pilot, led by director Maria Okafor and funded by the Greenfield Foundation, extended weekday hours to 9 pm for 16 weeks at a cost of $45,000. Weekday visits rose 21 percent, and card registrations grew from 310 to 365 per month. The pilot proved that evening hours work, and every neighbouring library is now planning to copy it. The county auditor noted that the pilot did measure whether new evening visitors kept using the library after the pilot ended. Staff reported that the late shift was hard to fill. The board will vote in November 2026 on whether to extend the hours through the 2027 budget year.
