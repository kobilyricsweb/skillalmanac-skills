Harbor Library Evening Hours Pilot

Background

The Harbor Library board approved a pilot in January 2026 that extended weekday closing time from 6 pm to 9 pm at the main branch. The pilot ran for 16 weeks and cost $48,200, mostly for additional staff. Library director Maria Okafor said the goal was to reach residents who work during the day.

Results

Evening visits averaged 212 per night, and weekday visits overall rose 18 percent compared with the same weeks in 2025. Study-room bookings after 6 pm reached 1,340 across the pilot. Adult card registrations grew from 310 to 365 per month, an increase of 18 percent. The survey of 604 patrons found that 71 percent of evening visitors had not visited the library before 6 pm in the previous year.

Concerns

Staff reported that the late shift was hard to fill. Three part-time employees left during the pilot, and overtime accounted for $9,100 of the total cost. The county auditor noted that the pilot did not measure whether new evening visitors kept using the library after the pilot ended, so the retention effect is unknown. Board member Daniel Reyes said the figures suggest the hours may be worth keeping, but he did not support a permanent change without a staffing plan.

Next steps

The board will vote in November 2026 on whether to extend the hours through the 2027 budget year. Okafor said the library will not hire permanent evening staff before the vote. The auditor recommended a follow-up survey six months after the pilot to measure retention.
