Riverside Bridge Repair Review

Summary of findings

The city engineer's review of the Riverside Bridge found corrosion in 14 of the 62 steel hangers that hold the deck. Inspectors rated the bridge 5 out of 9 on the national condition scale, down from 6 in 2023. The bridge carries about 21,000 vehicles per day.

Cost and schedule

Full replacement of all hangers is estimated at $7.4 million and would close the bridge for 9 months. A phased repair of only the 14 corroded hangers is estimated at $2.1 million and would require night closures for 5 weeks. Engineer Priya Nandakumar said the phased repair is adequate for roughly 10 years, though she added that the estimate assumes no further hangers fail inspection once the work begins.

Funding

The state has offered a grant covering 40 percent of the phased repair. The grant does not apply to a full replacement. The council would need to approve the remaining $1.26 million from the capital reserve, which currently holds $3.8 million.

Objections

Councilmember Tom Alvarez argued that a phased repair only delays the larger cost. He pointed out that the 2023 inspection found corrosion in only 6 hangers, so the problem more than doubled in two years. Nandakumar responded that the growth rate is uncertain because the 2023 inspection used a different method.

Decision

The council will vote on 3 December 2026. The staff recommendation is the phased repair, and a second inspection of all hangers is scheduled for March 2027 whatever the council decides.
