Required fields: evidence records; relevant dates; review or supersession rules; dependent-item links; and priority policy. Records should have stable identifiers and distinguish evidence from claims it supports. If a required field is missing, list the gap and leave affected items unscheduled or priority-undetermined; do not infer a date or status. Output fields: ordered review queue; evidence item; supplied rule; trigger date or unscheduled status; dependent claims; priority basis; interim label; open questions; and verification report. A review date means attention is due, not that the evidence is false. Apply supplied date conventions and show arithmetic for calculated dates. Do not update claims, declare supersession, or create reminders outside the artifact.\n
