# Decision log

One row per decision. Never edit a closed row; add a new row that supersedes it.

| ID | Date decided | Decision (one sentence, verb first) | Owner (a person) | Due | Reversible? | Review date | Evidence used | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 2026-11-16 | Pause the paid ads test | Dana | 2026-11-20 | yes | 2026-12-14 | CAC 3x target over 4 weeks (metrics sheet) | open |

Status values: open, done, superseded by D-nnn, dropped (say why in the Decision cell).
Reversible: yes means undoing it costs under one week of one person's time. Anything else is "no" and gets a review date no later than 30 days out.
