# Quiz: The Rise and Fall of Port Calder's Herring Trade

Grade 7 Social Studies

Name: ______________________  Date: ______________

Directions: Circle the one best answer for each question.

**1. Why did the first families settle Port Calder in 1764?**
A. They wanted to build a canal to Marrow
B. Herring gathered in Calder Bay every autumn
C. The regional government asked them to start a museum
D. They were looking for good farmland inland

**2. What was the first public building finished in Port Calder, and why was it built first?**
A. A town hall, so the council could pass rules
B. A market square, so fish could be sold
C. A salt store, because salt was needed to preserve the catch
D. A smokehouse, because the town had no road

**3. How did the canal that opened in 1823 change the herring trade?**
A. It cut the trip to Marrow from nine days to two, so fishermen could sell far more fish
B. It made the herring easier to catch in the bay
C. It closed the harbor to sailing boats
D. It made salt cheaper for the smokehouses

**4. About how many boats were in the fishing fleet in 1850?**
A. 40
B. 110
C. 210
D. 410

**5. What did the Net Rule of 1838 require?**
A. Boats had to stay in port for a week each month
B. Only steam-powered boats could fish in the bay
C. Fishermen had to sell their catch to the town council
D. Nets could not have a mesh smaller than one inch

**6. Why did most captains obey the Net Rule at first?**
A. The harbor master inspected nets on the dock and could hold a boat in port
B. The rule gave them higher pay
C. Young herring were easy to see and avoid
D. The coopers refused to make barrels for small fish

**7. Which decision in 1861 weakened the Net Rule?**
A. The council raised the tax on salt
B. The council abolished the post of harbor master
C. The council allowed steam boats into the harbor
D. The council changed the rule to allow smaller mesh

**8. Catches were highest in 1879, but the town was in trouble. What was the problem?**
A. The fish being caught were younger and smaller each year, and fewer survived to breed
B. The canal to Marrow had closed
C. There were too few boats to carry the fish
D. Herring had stopped coming to Calder Bay

**9. Which event happened in 1988?**
A. The last smokehouse closed
B. The old salt store became a museum
C. The population fell by half
D. The regional government protected Calder Bay as a breeding area

**10. According to the historians quoted in the reading, why did the Net Rule fail?**
A. It was a bad idea from the start
B. Steam boats were not covered by it
C. The town stopped paying someone to enforce it
D. The fishermen did not know about it

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - The reading says the families came for the herring that gathered in Calder Bay every autumn.
2. **C** - The salt store (1771) came first because without salt the catch could not be preserved for the trip to distant cities.
3. **A** - A barrel took nine days by cart before the canal and two days by barge after it.
4. **C** - The fleet grew from 40 boats in 1820 to 210 boats in 1850.
5. **D** - The Net Rule banned nets with a mesh smaller than one inch so young herring could escape and grow.
6. **A** - The harbor master inspected nets on the dock and could hold a boat in port for a week.
7. **B** - The council abolished the harbor master post in 1861 to save money, and nobody enforced the rule after that.
8. **A** - Catches rose at first, but the fish were younger and smaller each year, and by 1890 the catch was less than a quarter of the 1879 record.
9. **D** - In 1988 the regional government declared Calder Bay a protected breeding area with no commercial fishing.
10. **C** - The historians say the rule failed because the town stopped paying someone to enforce it, not because it was a bad idea.
