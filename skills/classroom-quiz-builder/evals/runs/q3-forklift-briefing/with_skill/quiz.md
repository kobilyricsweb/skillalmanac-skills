# Dunmore Warehouse Knowledge Check: Pallet Trucks and Forklifts

## Q1 [recall]
For how long is a site permit valid?
A. 3 months
B. 6 months
C. 12 months
D. 24 months
Answer: C
Source: "A permit is issued by the shift supervisor after a practical test and is valid for 12 months."
Why: C is right; the other durations do not appear in the briefing.

## Q2 [apply]
A new hire has a forklift licence from a previous employer. What must happen before they drive at Dunmore?
A. The supervisor must see them drive
B. They only need to complete this briefing
C. They must wait 12 months before applying
D. Their old licence is accepted straight away
Answer: A
Source: "A licence from a previous employer does not count until the supervisor has seen you drive."
Why: A is right; D is the tempting wrong option but the old licence does not count by itself.

## Q3 [recall]
Below what battery charge level must you take a powered truck to the charging bay instead of starting a job?
A. 5 percent
B. 10 percent
C. 15 percent
D. 20 percent
Answer: D
Source: "If the charge is below 20 percent, take the truck to the charging bay; do not start a job with it."
Why: D is right; 15 appears in the briefing only as the fork height in centimetres.

## Q4 [apply]
A forklift starts to tip over while you are driving it. What should you do?
A. Jump off quickly and run clear of the truck
B. Stay seated, hold the wheel, and lean away from the fall
C. Stay seated, let go of the wheel, and lean toward the fall
D. Raise the forks to shift the weight back
Answer: B
Source: "stay in the seat, hold the wheel, and lean away from the fall. Do not jump."
Why: B is right; jumping is the most tempting wrong option and the briefing says not to do it.

## Q5 [apply]
During the pre-use check you find the brakes on a truck are faulty. What should you do?
A. Fix it yourself if the fault looks small, then carry on
B. Switch to another truck and tell nobody
C. Tag the truck and drive it to the charging bay
D. Tag it, remove the key, and tell the shift supervisor
Answer: D
Source: "remove the key, and tell the shift supervisor. Do not try to fix it yourself."
Why: D is right; A is wrong because the briefing says not to try to fix a fault yourself.

## Q6 [apply]
A truck driver reaches a spot where a blue lane crosses a green walkway and a pedestrian is waiting. What should the driver do?
A. Stop, because the pedestrian has right of way
B. Sound the horn and pass at 6 km/h
C. Wave the pedestrian back and keep going
D. Slow down and pass in the loading yard
Answer: A
Source: "the truck stops and the pedestrian has right of way, always."
Why: A is right; the horn is for blind corners and the strip curtains, and it never replaces stopping at a crossing.

## Q7 [apply]
You are carrying a load that blocks your view forward. What should you do?
A. Raise the forks so you can see under the load
B. Ask a colleague to ride along and guide you
C. Drive in reverse
D. Sound the horn and drive forward slowly
Answer: C
Source: "If the load blocks your view forward, drive in reverse."
Why: C is right; B is wrong because the briefing says never to carry a passenger.

## Q8 [recall]
What is the maximum load for the red trucks?
A. 1,500 kilograms
B. 2,500 kilograms
C. 3,500 kilograms
D. 5,000 kilograms
Answer: B
Source: "for the two red trucks it is 2,500 kilograms."
Why: B is right; 1,500 kilograms is the limit for the yellow trucks.

## Q9 [understand]
What is the reason for the 5 metre safety zone around the charging bay, where smoking and naked flames are kept out?
A. Batteries give off hydrogen while they charge
B. Charging batteries get too hot to touch
C. Smoke can damage the battery chargers
D. Smoke can hide the blue lanes from drivers
Answer: A
Source: "because batteries give off hydrogen while charging"
Why: A is right; the briefing gives the hydrogen as the only reason for the rule.

## Q10 [understand]
Why must you report even a light hit on the racking before anyone else uses that aisle?
A. Because the supervisor must repaint it before the shift ends
B. Because a driver who hits racking loses the permit for a year
C. Because only the red trucks can use that aisle afterward
D. Because damaged racking can fail later with no warning
Answer: D
Source: "Damaged racking can fail later without warning."
Why: D is right; the briefing gives no penalty for the permit, so B is wrong.
