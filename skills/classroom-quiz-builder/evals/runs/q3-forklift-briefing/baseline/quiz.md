For new warehouse staff. Based on the Dunmore site briefing. These are Dunmore site rules; other sites differ.

---

## Answer Key (supervisor copy)

1. **D** - Anyone who has completed the briefing may use a hand pallet truck. Powered trucks and forklifts need a current site permit.
2. **A** - The shift supervisor issues the permit after a practical test. It is valid for 12 months. A previous employer's licence does not count until the supervisor has seen you drive.
3. **B** - The first driver of each shift does the pre-use check, using the checklist hanging on the truck.
4. **C** - Below 20 percent charge, take the truck to the charging bay and do not start a job with it.
5. **A** - Attach a red "Do not use" tag, remove the key, and tell the shift supervisor. Do not try to fix it yourself.
6. **A** - 6 km/h inside the building (10 km/h applies in the loading yard).
7. **D** - The truck stops and the pedestrian has right of way, always.
8. **A** - Forks 15 cm off the floor and tilted back, never travel with the load raised, and reverse if the load blocks the view forward.
9. **D** - Stop and report to the supervisor before anyone else uses that aisle, because damaged racking can fail later without warning.
10. **B** - Stay in the seat, hold the wheel, lean away from the fall, and do not jump.

Suggested follow-up for the supervisor: the briefing also covers load limits (1,500 kg yellow trucks, 2,500 kg red trucks), level 5 racking (red trucks only), parking and charging rules, and the collision procedure (red emergency stop, extension 555, incident book). These are not in the 10 questions above, so go over them verbally.
