# Quiz: How Leaves Make Food

Grade 6 Science, Unit 4

Name: ______________________  Date: ______________

Directions: Circle the one best answer for each question.

**1. What are the two ingredients a plant uses for photosynthesis?**
A. Glucose and oxygen
B. Water and carbon dioxide
C. Starch and water
D. Oxygen and carbon dioxide

**2. What is the energy source for photosynthesis?**
A. Heat from the soil
B. Water
C. Light
D. Stored starch

**3. Which of these is a product of photosynthesis (including the leftover product)?**
A. Chlorophyll
B. Cellulose
C. Iodine
D. Oxygen

**4. How does carbon dioxide get into a leaf?**
A. Through the roots
B. Through tiny openings called stomata
C. Through the xylem
D. Through the phloem

**5. Why does photosynthesis slow down on a hot, dry afternoon?**
A. The guard cells close the stomata to save water, so less carbon dioxide gets in
B. The chloroplasts turn brown in the heat
C. The roots stop taking in light
D. The leaf makes extra oxygen, which blocks the ingredients

**6. Why do roots not make food by photosynthesis?**
A. They do not contain water
B. They are underground, where there is no starch
C. They are too thick for light to pass through
D. They have no chloroplasts

**7. In the class experiment, part of a geranium leaf was covered with black paper for two days and then tested with iodine. What did the result show?**
A. The leaf made starch only where light reached it
B. The leaf made oxygen only where it was covered
C. The leaf made starch only where it was covered
D. The leaf stores starch in the dark

**8. Short answer: A potato is mostly stored starch. Name one other thing a plant can do with the glucose it makes.**

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Water and carbon dioxide are the ingredients.
2. **C** - Plants use the energy of light.
3. **D** - The products are glucose and oxygen. Chlorophyll is a pigment, cellulose is made from glucose afterward, and iodine is the test chemical.
4. **B** - Carbon dioxide enters through stomata, mostly on the underside of the leaf. Water, not carbon dioxide, travels through the xylem.
5. **A** - When the plant is short of water the guard cells go limp and close the stomata, which saves water but lets in less carbon dioxide.
6. **D** - Roots have no chloroplasts, so they are not green and cannot make food.
7. **A** - The uncovered part turned blue-black (starch present) and the covered part stayed yellow-brown (no starch).
8. **Any one of the following (full credit):** use it straight away for energy; join it into starch and store it (in roots or seeds); turn it into cellulose for cell walls; move it to other parts of the plant through the phloem. Do not accept "turn it into oxygen" or "make water," since oxygen is a leftover product made during photosynthesis, not something made from the sugar afterward.
