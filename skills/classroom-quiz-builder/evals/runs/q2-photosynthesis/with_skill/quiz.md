# How Leaves Make Food

## Q1 [recall]
Which two ingredients does a plant take in for photosynthesis?
A. Glucose and oxygen
B. Light and glucose
C. Water and carbon dioxide
D. Oxygen and carbon dioxide
Answer: C
Source: "A plant takes in two simple ingredients, water and carbon dioxide"
Why: C is right; glucose and oxygen are the products, and light is the energy source rather than an ingredient.

## Q2 [recall]
Which tubes carry water up the stem?
A. Xylem
B. Phloem
C. Stomata
D. Chloroplasts
Answer: A
Source: "travels up the stem in narrow tubes called xylem"
Why: A is right; phloem is the tempting wrong option but it carries sugar.

## Q3 [understand]
Why does photosynthesis slow down on a hot, dry afternoon?
A. The leaf cells make too much starch to keep working
B. The chloroplasts stop absorbing red and blue light
C. The roots take in more water than the leaf can use
D. The stomata close to save water, so less carbon dioxide gets in
Answer: D
Source: "This saves water, but it also means less carbon dioxide gets in, so photosynthesis slows down on a hot, dry afternoon."
Why: D is right; the plant is short of water, not flooded with it, so C is the reverse of the notes.

## Q4 [understand]
Why do leaves look green?
A. Chlorophyll absorbs green light and reflects red and blue light
B. Chlorophyll reflects green light, and we see that reflected light
C. Starch stored in the leaf cells is green in colour
D. Xylem tubes carrying water through the leaf are green
Answer: B
Source: "a pigment that absorbs red and blue light and reflects green light. That reflected green light is what we see."
Why: B is right; A swaps which colours are absorbed and which are reflected.

## Q5 [apply]
A student looks at a plant cell under a microscope and finds it has no chloroplasts. What can she conclude about that cell?
A. It cannot store any starch
B. It cannot take in any water
C. It makes glucose only at night
D. It cannot carry out photosynthesis
Answer: D
Source: "Photosynthesis happens inside chloroplasts"
Why: D is right; roots have no chloroplasts yet store starch and take in water, so A and B are wrong.

## Q6 [understand]
What did the iodine test on the geranium leaf show?
A. The leaf made starch only where it was covered
B. The leaf made starch only where light reached it
C. The leaf made starch evenly across its whole surface
D. The covered part of the leaf made twice as much starch
Answer: B
Source: "This shows that the leaf made starch only where light reached it."
Why: B is right; the covered part stayed yellow-brown, so it had no starch, which rules out A and D.

## Q7 [apply]
A plant is placed in a dark room overnight. What does the lesson say will happen?
A. Photosynthesis stops, but the plant keeps using stored sugar for energy
B. Photosynthesis speeds up because the plant uses its stored starch
C. The plant stops using sugar until light returns
D. The plant releases extra oxygen until morning
Answer: A
Source: "At night there is no light, so photosynthesis stops, but the plant keeps using stored sugar for energy."
Why: A is right; oxygen is released by photosynthesis, which has stopped, so D is wrong.

## Q8 [understand]
In terms of the lesson, what is a potato?
A. A bundle of phloem tubes that carry sugar
B. A group of cells packed with chloroplasts
C. Mostly starch made from glucose and stored
D. Mostly cellulose from tough cell walls
Answer: C
Source: "Some is joined into long chains called starch and stored, often in roots or seeds. A potato is mostly stored starch."
Why: C is right; cellulose is made from some glucose too, but the notes say the potato is mostly starch.
