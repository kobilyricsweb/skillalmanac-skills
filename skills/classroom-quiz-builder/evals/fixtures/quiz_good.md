# Port Calder herring trade: check quiz

## Q1 [recall]
What was the first public building in Port Calder?
A. The town hall
B. The salt store
C. The smokehouse
D. The museum
Answer: B
Source: "The first public building, finished in 1771, was the salt store"
Why: The reading names the salt store; the town had no town hall for its first forty years.

## Q2 [understand]
Why did the herring fleet grow so much after 1823?
A. The council paid fishermen to build boats
B. Steam boats could fish in bad weather
C. A new harbor master protected the bay
D. The canal made transport cheaper and faster
Answer: D
Source: "Cheaper and faster transport meant that fishermen could sell far more fish"
Why: The canal opened in 1823; steam boats came later, in 1874.

## Q3 [recall]
What did the Net Rule of 1838 ban?
A. Nets with a mesh smaller than one inch
B. Fishing in Calder Bay during autumn
C. Boats with more than three sails
D. Selling herring outside the town
Answer: A
Source: "It banned nets with a mesh smaller than one inch"
Why: The rule was about mesh size so young herring could escape.

## Q4 [understand]
According to the reading, why did the Net Rule stop working?
A. Captains voted to cancel the rule
B. The rule only applied to steam boats
C. Nobody was paid to enforce it any more
D. Young herring learned to avoid nets
Answer: C
Source: "The Net Rule failed not because it was a bad idea, but because the town stopped paying someone to enforce it"
Why: The harbor master post was abolished in 1861; the rule itself was never cancelled.

## Q5 [apply]
A town today has a good fishing rule but plans to cut the inspector's job to save money. Based on Port Calder's story, what is most likely to happen?
A. The rule will be ignored within a few seasons
B. Fishermen will ask for a stricter rule
C. The fish will move to another bay
D. Catches will fall in the first year
Answer: A
Source: "nobody enforced it, and small-mesh nets returned within a few seasons"
Why: Port Calder kept its rule on the books but lost enforcement; catches rose at first, so D is wrong.
