# Site briefing: pallet trucks and forklifts at the Dunmore warehouse

*For new warehouse staff. Read before your first shift. Your supervisor will go through a knowledge check with you. These are Dunmore site rules; other sites differ.*

## Who may drive what

Anyone who has completed this briefing may use a hand pallet truck. Powered pallet trucks and forklifts may be driven only by staff who hold a current site permit. A permit is issued by the shift supervisor after a practical test and is valid for 12 months. A licence from a previous employer does not count until the supervisor has seen you drive.

## Before you start

Every powered truck gets a pre-use check at the start of each shift, done by the first driver of that shift. The checklist hangs on the truck. Check the horn, brakes, forks, chains, tyres and the battery charge. If the charge is below 20 percent, take the truck to the charging bay; do not start a job with it. If you find a fault, attach a red "Do not use" tag, remove the key, and tell the shift supervisor. Do not try to fix it yourself.

## Speed and routes

The speed limit inside the building is 6 km/h, about a fast walk. In the loading yard it is 10 km/h. Trucks use the blue lanes. Pedestrians use the green walkways. Where a blue lane crosses a green walkway, the truck stops and the pedestrian has right of way, always. Sound the horn at every blind corner and at the plastic strip curtains between Hall A and Hall B.

## Carrying loads

Travel with the forks 15 centimetres off the floor, tilted back. Never travel with the load raised. If the load blocks your view forward, drive in reverse. The maximum load for the yellow trucks is 1,500 kilograms; for the two red trucks it is 2,500 kilograms. The capacity plate on the truck is the final word. Never carry a passenger, and never lift a person on the forks.

## Racking

The top racking level, level 5, is for the red trucks only. Yellow trucks may serve levels 1 to 4. Place the heaviest pallets on the lowest levels. If you hit the racking, even lightly, stop and report it to the supervisor before anyone else uses that aisle. Damaged racking can fail later without warning.

## Parking and charging

Park only in a marked bay, forks lowered flat to the floor, handbrake on, key removed. Never park in front of a fire exit or an extinguisher. Charging is done only in the charging bay at the east end of Hall B. No smoking or naked flames within 5 metres of the charging bay, because batteries give off hydrogen while charging.

## If something goes wrong

If a truck starts to tip, stay in the seat, hold the wheel, and lean away from the fall. Do not jump. For any collision with a person, press the nearest red emergency stop button, call first aid on extension 555, and do not move the truck. All incidents and near misses are written in the incident book in the supervisor's office before the end of the shift.
