[Verse 1]
The kettle sings, the street is gray
The neighbors hurry on their way
Where lonely sailors used to roam
I trust the light that leads me home

[Verse 2]
The tide comes in, the shutters sway
A gull complains about the day
I count the boats that cross the foam
I trust the light that leads me home

[Bridge]
If every window turned to stone
And every road forgot my name
I'd walk it still, I'd walk alone
And knock on doors until they came

[Verse 3]
The morning mends the broken gray
The children laugh and run and play
Wherever sailors used to roam
I trust the light that leads me home
