# approval-path-mapper

Unpublished original review draft. Editorial tier: free. No Platform Score or public sale.

## One Line

Turn supplied authority rules into an auditable approval sequence.

## Audience

Operations leads, governance owners, and workflow designers.

## Deliverable

Role-based approval path with branches, evidence handoffs, escalations, and ambiguity or cycle flags.

## Limits

Uses supplied rules only; does not grant authority, approve artifacts, schedule reviews, or execute workflows.
