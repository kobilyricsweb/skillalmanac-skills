## Where to verify each fact kind

| Fact kind | What can change | Check at |
|---|---|---|
| hours | seasonal hours, weekly closing day, public holidays, last-entry time, timed-entry slots | the venue's own site or ticket page, for the exact date |
| price | season, age bands, residents versus visitors, booking fees, currency | the venue's or seller's own page; note the date |
| visa | depends on passport, length of stay, purpose, transit, previous stays | the destination government's entry page or its embassy; the traveller's own passport country |
| booking | how far ahead a slot opens, whether free cancellation exists | the seller's page and the cancellation terms |
| transport | timetables, strikes, works, transfer times | the carrier or transit operator for the date |
| other | anything else stated as fact | the most official source available |

A source is the URL read in this session. A forum post or an earlier answer is not a source for a verified fact.

## Document checklist by trip type

Mark each as `have`, `todo` or `unverified`. Anything whose rule depends on the destination stays `unverified` until the official source is read.

International trip:
- Passports for every traveller; validity rule of the destination and any required blank pages (unverified)
- Visa or electronic travel authorisation, if any (unverified)
- Return or onward ticket, since carriers sometimes ask for it (unverified)
- Travel insurance policy and emergency number
- Proof of accommodation for the first night
- Health requirements and any prescriptions in original packaging (unverified, rules differ)
- Some local currency and one backup payment card kept separate

Driving:
- Driving licence; whether an international permit is needed (unverified)
- Rental booking, insurance cover and card used for the deposit
- Vehicle papers, toll or vignette rules, low-emission zone rules (unverified)

With children:
- Each child's passport or ID; consent letter when travelling with one parent or non-parents (unverified)
- Booster or car-seat rules (unverified)

Cruise or rail pass:
- Boarding documents and the latest check-in time
- Pass activation rules and reservation requirements per train (unverified)

Domestic trip: ID, tickets, booking confirmations, insurance if any.

## Wording for unverified facts

Use "unverified, check <source> for <date>" in tables and keep the claim itself in the to-verify list. Do not soften by writing "usually", "around" or "typically" as a way to state a number; either give the user-provided or sourced figure with its date, or leave the figure out.
