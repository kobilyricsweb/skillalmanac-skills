Required inputs are two count lists; stable item identifiers or an explicit matching rule; count times; documented adjustments such as supplied sales or transfers; and permitted resolution steps. Each list should provide the observed value and, where available, its unit or location. Missing identifiers, times, adjustment evidence, or permissions must be marked unknown and may block classification for affected rows. Similar names do not establish a match.

Return fields for each row: item identifier or ambiguity note, first observation, second observation, count times, difference, documented adjustment, arithmetic explanation, classification, evidence citation, and proposed permitted next step. Include unresolved inputs and a review summary. Preserve observations exactly, do not infer shrinkage or corrections, and do not modify source records. A difference is explainable only when supplied evidence and arithmetic support it.
