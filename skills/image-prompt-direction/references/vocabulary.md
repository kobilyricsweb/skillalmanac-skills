# Vocabulary: what each term does

Read only the section for the field you are choosing terms for.

## Shot size and angle

| Term | Effect |
|---|---|
| extreme close-up | one detail fills the frame; no context |
| close-up | head and shoulders, or one object; intimate |
| medium shot | waist up, or an object with some surroundings; default for people |
| wide shot | subject small inside the setting; sets place |
| low angle | camera below the subject; looks powerful or monumental |
| high angle / top-down | camera above; looks small, mapped, or tidy |
| eye level | neutral, documentary |
| rule of thirds | subject on a third line, empty space on the other side |
| centred, symmetrical | formal, iconic, posters |
| negative space | deliberate empty area, useful when text will be added later |

## Lens and depth of field

| Term | Effect |
|---|---|
| 14 to 28 mm, wide-angle | stretches space, exaggerates near objects; use for interiors and landscapes |
| 35 mm | natural street and documentary view |
| 50 mm | close to the eye; neutral |
| 85 mm | flattering portraits, gentle background blur |
| 135 mm and longer, telephoto | compresses distance; stacked layers; distant subjects |
| macro (often 100 mm) | tiny subjects at life size |
| f/1.4 to f/2.8 | shallow depth, blurred background |
| f/8 to f/11 | most of the scene sharp |
| f/16 and smaller | everything sharp, landscape |
| long exposure | smooth water, light trails |

"16 mm film" and "35 mm film" name a film gauge, not a lens. The script does not treat a number followed by "film" as a focal length.

## Lighting

| Term | Effect |
|---|---|
| golden hour | low warm side light, long shadows, shortly after sunrise or before sunset |
| blue hour | cool, dim, even sky just before sunrise or after sunset |
| overcast | soft shadowless light, muted colour |
| midday or noon sun | hard overhead light, short harsh shadows |
| window light | soft directional light from one side; indoor portraits |
| rim or back light | bright edge outlining the subject; separates from background |
| softbox, diffused | even soft light, gentle shadows |
| hard light | crisp, dark-edged shadows; drama, texture |
| high-key | bright, low contrast, few shadows |
| low-key, chiaroscuro | mostly dark with a pool of light; moody |
| volumetric | visible beams in haze or dust |

## Palette

Name three to five colours, or one relationship: complementary (opposites, high energy), analogous (neighbours, calm), monochrome (one hue, tonal), muted or desaturated (greyed, quiet), limited with one accent (mostly neutral, one saturated colour). Colour names with a material work better than abstract hues: `brick red`, `celadon`, `charcoal`.

## Style and medium

Say the medium and then the finish: `gouache illustration, matte flat fills, visible brush texture`; `documentary photograph, fine film grain, natural skin texture`; `isometric 3D render, soft clay material, studio backdrop`; `woodblock print, limited ink colours, paper texture`. A movement name (art deco, bauhaus, ukiyo-e) is a safe shorthand because it names a period, not a person.
