# Brief

Client: Oat & Ember, a neighbourhood bakery.
Use: hero image at the top of the new website, full width.
Mood: warm, early morning, unhurried. Customers should want to walk in.
Must show: a baker pulling a tray of loaves from a stone oven. The shop window is visible behind.
Colours from the brand: cream, burnt orange, deep brown.
Nothing with text on it; the site adds its own headline over the left side of the image.
