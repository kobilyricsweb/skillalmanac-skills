# Introduction

Student attention drops quickly after the first 20 minutes of a lecture [@marsh2016]. Phones are blamed for a large share of that loss, and 71% of teachers report banning them at least once. Most of them say it helps. Policy research is thin [@okafor2020], so the evidence base is still developing.

# Methods

We observed 6 classrooms over 4 weeks. Attention was coded every 5 minutes by two raters.

# Results

Attention scores were 3.9 points higher in phone-free rooms (see Table 1).

# Discussion

The gain matches earlier lab findings [@marsh2016; @tanaka2018]. Class sizes above 30 pupils may weaken the effect.
