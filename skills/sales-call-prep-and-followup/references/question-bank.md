## How to use the bank

Pick one opening question per stage, then at most one probe per stage when the answer is thin. Rewrite in the prospect's vocabulary from the notes. The bank is a starting set, not a script: 5 to 8 questions in total.

## Situation

Open:
- Walk me through how this gets done today, from the moment it starts.
- Who is involved, and which tools do they touch?
- How much of it is there: per day, per month, per team?

Probe if thin: "Which part of that takes the most time?" Note the volume with its unit.

## Problem

Open:
- Where does it go wrong?
- When did that last happen? What happened next?
- Who notices first?

Probe if the answer is a feature wish: "What would that let you do that you cannot do now?" Record the failure, not the wish.

## Impact

Open:
- What does that cost you, in money, hours or lost deals, in a typical month?
- What happens if nothing changes this year?
- What else is that delaying?

Probe if no number: "Roughly, is it closer to hundreds or thousands?" or "Who would know the figure?" Write the figure with its source in the notes ("Priya, estimate"). If nobody can supply one, record that.

## Decision process

Open:
- Besides you, who else would weigh in?
- How has your team bought something like this before?
- What would have to be true for this to be a yes this quarter?
- Is anything else being considered, including doing nothing?

Probe for the gaps the checker looks for: who approves, by when, budget or paperwork (security review, procurement, legal). One question each, only for what is still missing.

## Next step

Open:
- Based on what we covered, what would be useful to do next?
- Who should be on that, and what day works?

Close by saying the next step back with the owner and the date, and put it in the notes before hanging up.

## Reading thin answers

| They said | Record as | Probe |
|---|---|---|
| "It's fine, mostly" | situation, unanswered problem | "When was the last time it was not fine?" |
| "Costs us a lot" | impact, no number | the hundreds-or-thousands probe |
| "I'd need to check with my boss" | decision process, approver unknown | "What would your boss want to see first?" |
| "Send me something" | next step not agreed | propose a dated step; if refused, record "no next step, prospect declined" |
