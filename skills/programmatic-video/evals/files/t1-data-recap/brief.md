# Weekly recap video

Destination: vertical short video, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 15 seconds total. Every file lives under `assets/` next to the timeline.

Scenes, in order:

1. 0 to 5 s: `assets/title-card.png` (1080x1920 image, own graphic).
2. 5 to 11 s: `assets/chart-anim.mp4` (video, 1080x1920, 25 fps, 8.0 s long, own footage).
3. 11 to 15 s: `assets/outro-card.png` (1080x1920 image, own graphic).

Music: `assets/bed-loop.mp3`, 60 s long, licence "CC0, library track 22". Plays the whole video.

Captions (all inside the safe area, one box at x 90, y 1400, w 900, h 200):

- 0.5 s for 3.5 s: "This week in numbers"
- 5.5 s for 3 s: "Signups rose 12 percent"
- 8.5 s for 2.5 s: "Churn stayed flat at\n3 percent"
- 11.5 s for 3 s: "Thanks for watching"

Save the timeline as `timeline.json` in the project root.
