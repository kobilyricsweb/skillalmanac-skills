# Warehouse Pick-Rate Review

## Summary
Average pick rate rose from 92 to 108 lines per hour between January and June after the new slotting layout. Error rate stayed flat at 0.4 percent.

## Method
Data came from the warehouse system export, one row per pick line, 14 weeks per half-year.

### Data cleaning
Rows with a duration under 2 seconds were removed (1,140 rows).

## Results

| Month | Lines per hour | Error rate % |
|---|---|---|
| January | 92 | 0.4 |
| March | 101 | 0.4 |
| June | 108 | 0.4 |

![Pick rate by month, rising from 92 to 108](pickrate.png)

#### Zone detail
Zone C improved most; zone A did not change.

## Recommendations
Extend the layout to the second building. Budget and dates are not yet agreed.
