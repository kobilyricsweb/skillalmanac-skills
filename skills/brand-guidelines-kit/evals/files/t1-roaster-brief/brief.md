# Brief: Tern Coffee Roasters

We roast single-origin coffee in small batches in Leeds and sell it by subscription to home brewers in the UK who are tired of supermarket beans. We also run a small tasting room open at weekends.

Comparable brands we like: Square Mile, Origin, Pact. Brands we are not like: Starbucks, Nespresso.

We have a wordmark as an SVG (dark green on white, no reversed version yet) and no other files. We have not chosen fonts. Colours so far: a dark green from the wordmark, roughly #1F4D3A. Nothing else is fixed.

It will be used on the website, bag labels (print), Instagram and a wholesale price sheet. No proof points have been collected yet beyond "roasted within 7 days of dispatch".
