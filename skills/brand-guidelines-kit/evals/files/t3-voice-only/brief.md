# Brief: Ledgerly

Ledgerly is invoicing and expense software for freelance designers and photographers in the US. Our customers dread tax season and are suspicious of finance jargon. Competitors: FreshBooks, Wave, QuickBooks.

Current website copy example: "Streamline your financial workflows with our best-in-class invoicing solution."

We want a voice and tone guide only (no palette, no logo). Our support team also writes outage notices and refund replies.
