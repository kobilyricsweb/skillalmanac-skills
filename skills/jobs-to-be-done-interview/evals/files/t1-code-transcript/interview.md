# Interview 3 (P3, operations lead at a 40-person logistics firm), 52 minutes

I: Tell me about the last time you changed how you schedule driver shifts.
P: That was in October. We ran shifts off a shared spreadsheet for years. One Sunday night two drivers both showed up for the same route and nobody covered the cold-chain run, and the client called me at six in the morning.
I: What happened after that call?
P: I spent the morning on the phone fixing it. I told myself it was a one-off. We had a laminated rule for who updates the sheet, and honestly I trusted it, I'd built that routine myself.
I: When did you start looking at other options?
P: About a week later, when it happened again with a smaller client. A friend in another depot told me his dispatchers see open shifts on their phones and accept them with one tap. I wanted exactly that, so I'm not the one chasing people at night.
I: What did you do next?
P: I trialled two products over two weeks. I was nervous about the drivers, half of them don't like apps and two of them are over sixty. I also worried about what happens to our shift history when we move.
I: How did it go in the end?
P: We moved in November. The first Sunday nobody called me, and I slept through it. That is what I was after.
