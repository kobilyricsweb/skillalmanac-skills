# Client brief

Business: Fernhill Plants, an online shop selling houseplants from a nursery in Norwich, UK. Two staff.

Sender: Ruth at Fernhill Plants, ruth@fernhillplants.example. Registered address: Unit 4, Heron Yard, Norwich NR3 1AB, United Kingdom.

Who: people who create an account on fernhillplants.example. The signup form has an unticked box "Send me care tips and offers by email" and sends a confirmation link; the CRM stores the time and the form version. About 300 signups a month, UK and Ireland.

Goal: first order within 21 days of signup. Today about 1 in 9 new accounts orders.

Tool: Mailchimp-style platform that supports one-click unsubscribe headers.

Offer allowed: free UK delivery on the first order (normally 4.95 GBP) until day 14 of the account. No percentage discounts.

Wanted: an automated welcome sequence of up to 5 emails. Tone: friendly, plain, no hype. Do not claim stock levels or plant survival guarantees.
