Project Harbor status memo for the regional team.

The pilot began in March and covered three warehouses.

Inventory counts improved by about four percent during the first quarter.

Staff training was completed in two sessions held on weekdays.

The main risk is delayed delivery of replacement scanners.

Budget remains within the approved limit set in January.

Vendor contracts will be reviewed before the end of the month.

Two warehouses reported minor problems with label printers.

The steering group meets every other Tuesday at ten.

Feedback from floor supervisors has been mostly positive.

Next steps include a full audit of the pilot data.

Questions should be sent to the program office by Friday.
