Project Harbor status memo for the regional team.

The pilot began in April and covered three warehouses.

Vendor contracts will be reviewed before the end of the month.

The steering group meets every other Tuesday at ten.

Feedback from the floor supervisors has been very positive.

Next steps include a full audit of the pilot data.

A closing review will be held at the end of the second quarter.
