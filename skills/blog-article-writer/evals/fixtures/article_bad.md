# The Ultimate Guide to Writing Release Notes in the Modern Software Development Landscape of Today

In today's fast-paced world, release notes have become more important than ever. Have you ever wondered why nobody reads them? When it comes to communicating with your users, there is a lot to consider, and in this article we will explore everything you need to know about writing great release notes that your customers will love, and that will help your team ship faster and communicate better with everyone involved in the process, from engineers to support staff to executives who need to understand what changed, why it changed, and what it means for the business going forward in the coming quarters.

### Why they matter

Release notes matter a lot. Studies show that 73% of users read release notes, and companies save $2.4 million a year with better ones. Many experts agree.
