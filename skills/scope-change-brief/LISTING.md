# scope-change-brief

Unpublished original; editorial tier: free. No public score or release approval.

## What and when

Use when a supplied baseline and requested scope change must be compared through field-level deltas, impacts, criteria, approval scope, and unresolved decisions. Do not use for full PRDs, estimation, strategic selection, approving or implementing changes, or treating a title or reported approval as proof of authority.

## Task boundary

prd-writer drafts a complete product specification, project-tickets-writer creates implementation tickets, spec-driven-build implements an approved specification, and plan-stress-test challenges an execution plan; this compares a supplied baseline with a supplied change at field level, preserves unresolved baseline dispositions and approval scope, and creates no PRD, tickets, implementation or plan audit.
