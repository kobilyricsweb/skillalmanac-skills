# dataset-field-profiler

Status: unpublished free candidate; no score or verification badge.

## One Line

Describe observed top-level field coverage and JSON types across records.

## Audience

Data curators and pipeline maintainers.

## Benefit

Produces a value-free field inventory without turning variation into validation failures.

## Limits

Profiles observations only; it does not enforce a schema or infer meaning.

## Synthetic Example

Two records can show `year` as one integer and one fractional number while remaining valid.
