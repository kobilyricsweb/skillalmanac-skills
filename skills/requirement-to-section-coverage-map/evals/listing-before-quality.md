Audience: authors, reviewers, and publication teams. Practical limits: checks only supplied requirements and document content; it does not create an outline or supply missing evidence.

Unpublished editorial tier: free. No score or release approval.

Scope distinction: Nearest is source-note-to-outline; this audits a completed document against explicit content requirements rather than constructing an outline from source notes.
