# Fictionville curbside composting pilot: what the record shows

## Question

What did the Fictionville curbside composting pilot achieve, and is it continuing? Scope: public documents dated 2026. Fictional data, for testing only.

## Summary

The pilot kept a large volume of food waste out of landfill and was extended by the council [C1, C2].

## Findings

The Waste Office reports that the pilot diverted 412 tonnes of food waste between March and August 2026 [C1]. It covered 6,200 households [C5].

The council voted to extend the pilot through June 2027 [C2].

Household participation is reported at about 58 percent by one source and 61 percent by another [C3, C4]. The two figures differ in date and counting method, so this report gives a range rather than one number.
