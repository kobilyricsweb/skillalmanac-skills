# Churn notes (Northwind Cloud, B2B SaaS)

- Monthly logo churn: 1.9% in Q2, 3.8% in Q3 (billing system export, 2026-10-05).
- Churn by tenure in Q3: customers under 90 days old 9.4%, older customers 1.6% (same export).
- Q3 new-customer cohort is 40% larger than Q2, mostly from a paid-ads push in July (marketing report).
- Support tickets per customer unchanged at 2.1 per month (helpdesk report).
- Competitor cut list price by 15% in August (one sales rep says so; not confirmed).
- Price of our plans unchanged since January.
- Exit survey (31 answers of 118 leavers): 14 said "not enough use", 9 "too expensive", 8 other.
