# Facts for a market-entry question (Harbor Foods, packaged soup, 12 million revenue)

Question: should Harbor Foods launch in the Nordic market next year?

- Nordic packaged-soup market: about 310 million, growing 2% a year (industry report, 2025 edition).
- Harbor's domestic share is 9%, built over 11 years.
- Two incumbents hold 58% of Nordic sales between them (same industry report).
- Retail listing fees in the Nordics are around 8% of first-year sales (one retailer buyer, interview).
- Harbor's gross margin is 34%; shipping to the Nordics would cost about 6 points of margin (logistics quote, dated last month).
- Launch budget approved: 1.5 million. Management hopes for 3% share within three years (internal slide, no method).
