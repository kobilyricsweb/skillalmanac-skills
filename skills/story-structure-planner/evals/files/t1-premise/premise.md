# Premise

Genre: contemporary mystery, third person past tense, one point of view throughout.
Length target: about 30,000 words.

Nell Aldous is a night-shift archivist at a small-town newspaper that is about to close. She wants to save her job by finding a story big enough to keep the paper alive. What she needs is to stop hiding behind other people's records and put her own name on something. While digitising old photographs she finds a 1998 picture of the mayor at the scene of a fire that the official report says he never attended. If she publishes and is wrong, the paper is sued shut and she loses her reference. If she says nothing, the archive is scheduled to be destroyed on 30 June.

Other people: Dev Marlow, the editor, who is tired and kind; Mayor Ruth Okafor, the antagonist; Tomas, a retired fire inspector who may know the truth.
