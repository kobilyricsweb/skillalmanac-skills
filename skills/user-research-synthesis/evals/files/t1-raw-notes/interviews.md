# Meal-kit app, 5 interviews (research question: why do subscribers skip or cancel?)

## P1 (subscriber 8 months)
- "The delivery window is four hours and I am never home for it."
- "I tried to skip a week and it charged me anyway, so now I do not trust the skip button."
- Interviewer noted: P1 sighed when opening the settings page.

## P2 (subscriber 3 months)
- "Twice the box came at nine at night, which is useless for fresh food."
- "Some recipes take over an hour, I thought this was supposed to be thirty minutes."

## P3 (subscriber 1 year)
- "I could not tell whether skipping a week was done, there was no confirmation."
- "The long recipes are the reason I stopped cooking from the box."

## P4 (cancelled after 2 months)
- "The delivery window never matched my work schedule, that is why I left."
- "Customer support answered in two days, which was fine."

## P5 (subscriber 5 months)
- "I skipped a week last month and got an email later saying it was not processed, so I was billed."
- "The delivery window has been fine for me, it always arrives before noon."
