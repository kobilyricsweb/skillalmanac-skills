Remote work is available to every employee after the first ninety days of employment. Employees agree their working hours with their manager and record them in the time system by the end of each week. Equipment such as a laptop, a monitor and a headset is ordered through the facilities team, and it remains the property of the company. When an employee leaves, all equipment must be returned within ten working days, and the company covers the cost of courier shipping for remote staff.

Travel must be approved by a manager before any booking is made. Economy class is the standard for flights under six hours, and premium economy is allowed above that limit. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the city rate published by the finance team each January. Receipts must be uploaded within thirty days of the trip, and claims submitted later than sixty days after the trip are not paid.

Annual leave is twenty-five days per year plus public holidays. Unused days up to five may be carried into the next year, and the carry-over expires on the thirty-first of March. Sick leave does not reduce annual leave, and a medical certificate is required after three consecutive days of absence. Parental leave follows the statutory schedule, and the company tops up statutory pay to full salary for the first sixteen weeks.

Security incidents, including lost devices and suspected phishing emails, must be reported to the security desk within one hour of discovery. Passwords are managed through the company password manager, and sharing credentials by chat or email is a disciplinary matter. Laptops use full disk encryption, and the security team can wipe a lost device remotely.
