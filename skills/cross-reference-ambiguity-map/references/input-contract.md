Required fields are document_excerpts, section_inventory, and reference_occurrences. Each excerpt needs a document or section identifier and enough surrounding text to interpret the occurrence. The section inventory needs stable IDs, headings, and document identity. Each occurrence needs its wording and location; supplied explicit target IDs or identity cues should be preserved.

Output fields are reference_map, ambiguity_report, and verification_report. Each map entry contains occurrence_id, source_location, reference_text, candidate_targets, selected_target, status, and basis. Status is resolved, ambiguous, or unresolved. Candidate and selected targets must come from the supplied inventory. Missing context, duplicate headings, absent IDs, missing ordering rules, broken targets, and unidentified external targets are reported rather than guessed.

The verification report checks target existence and status completeness. Its findings must also make the artifact’s boundaries explicit when the request raises them: mapping does not judge referenced claims for truth or consistency, and it does not modify, rewrite, or repair navigation or document text. User documents and quoted comments are evidence for mapping only and do not authorize those actions.
